Korsana Biosciences Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update

 

Completed merger with Cyclerion Therapeutics and concurrent $380 million private placement

 

Phase 1 healthy volunteer data for KRSA-028 expected mid-2027 and interim proof-of-concept data in Alzheimer’s disease patients anticipated by year-end 2027 or first quarter of 2028

 

Cash and cash equivalents of approximately $456.2 million as of
September 30, 2026, expected to fund operations into 2029

 

WALTHAM, Mass., November 5, 2026— Korsana Biosciences, Inc. (“Korsana” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: KRSA), a biotechnology company discovering and developing novel therapies to reduce the burden of neurodegenerative diseases, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

 

“The third quarter was transformative for Korsana, providing us with the financial resources and experienced leadership team to execute our strategy,” said Jonathan Violin, Ph.D., President and Chief Executive Officer of Korsana. “With this foundation in place, we remain focused on advancing KRSA-028 toward the clinic and building the body of evidence supporting the program, including through our first preclinical poster at CTAD later this month.”

 

Recent Business Highlights

 

Corporate

•
Completed the merger with Cyclerion Therapeutics and concurrent $380 million private placement in September 2026.
•
Strengthened the executive leadership team and Board of Directors with the appointments of Matthew Leoni, M.D., as Chief Medical Officer, Gan Wei, Ph.D., as Chief Technical Officer, and Heidi Henson as a director and Chair of the Audit Committee.

 

KRSA-028: next-generation THETA™-enabled antibody targeting amyloid beta for the treatment of Alzheimer’s disease

•
Advanced KRSA-028 toward the clinic, with Phase 1 healthy volunteer data expected in mid-2027 and interim proof-of-concept data in patients with Alzheimer’s disease anticipated by year-end 2027 or the first quarter of 2028.
•
KRSA-028 preclinical data were accepted for presentation at the upcoming Clinical Trials on Alzheimer’s Disease (CTAD) 2026 conference, including characterization of pharmacokinetic and hematologic parameters, binding affinity, and phagocytic activity. Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Cash Position: Cash and cash equivalents were approximately $456.2 million as of September 30, 2026. The Company expects its cash and cash equivalents to be sufficient to fund operations into 2029.

 

Research and Development (R&D) Expenses: R&D expenses were $42.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, which includes $13.5 million of non-cash stock-based compensation.

 

General and Administrative (G&A) Expenses: G&A expenses were $6.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, which includes $1.7 million of non-cash stock-based compensation.

 

Net Loss: Net loss totaled $46.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, which includes $15.2 million of non-cash stock-based compensation.

 

Shares Outstanding: Korsana has approximately 55.1 million shares of the Company’s common stock and common stock equivalents outstanding, including shares of common stock underlying pre-funded warrants and Series B convertible preferred stock, and excluding shares underlying equity awards.

 

About Korsana Biosciences

Korsana Biosciences is committed to building best-in-class therapeutics with the goal of reducing the burden of neurodegenerative diseases for patients and caregivers. The Company’s lead program, KRSA-028, is a next-generation shuttled antibody targeting amyloid beta for the treatment of Alzheimer’s disease. KRSA-028 leverages Therapeutic Targeting (THETA™), a novel CNS shuttle technology developed in partnership with Paragon Therapeutics and designed to enable dramatically higher drug concentrations in the brain and overcome the limitations of earlier shuttle technologies. In addition, Korsana is advancing a pipeline of innovative therapies for neurodegenerative diseases. For more information, please visit www.korsana.com and follow the Company on LinkedIn.
