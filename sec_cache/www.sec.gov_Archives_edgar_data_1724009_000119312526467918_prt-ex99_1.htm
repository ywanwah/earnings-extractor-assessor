PermRock Royalty Trust

Declares Monthly Cash Distribution

 

DALLAS, Texas, November 19, 2026 – PermRock Royalty Trust (NYSE:PRT) (the “Trust”) today declared a monthly cash distribution to record holders of its trust units representing beneficial interests in the Trust (“Trust Units”) as of November 30, 2026, and payable on December 14, 2026, in the amount of $103,164.56 ($0.008479 per Trust Unit), based principally upon production during the month of September 2026.

Oil cash receipts for the properties underlying the Trust totaled $1.18 million for the current month, an increase of $0.17 million from the prior month’s distribution period. T2S Permian Acquisition II LLC (“T2S”) informed the Trust that this increase was primarily due to an increase in oil sales prices. Natural gas cash receipts for the properties underlying the Trust totaled $0.05 million for the current month, an increase of approximately $0.01 million from the prior month’s distribution period, primarily due to an increase in natural gas sales prices, partially offset by a decrease in natural gas sales volumes. Other revenue totaled $0.01 million for the current month, including salt water disposal income.

Total direct operating expenses, including marketing, lease operating expenses, and workover expenses, were $0.54 million, an increase of approximately $0.08 million from the prior month’s distribution period. T2S informed the Trust that this increase was primarily due to increases in workover expenses and lease operating expenses.

Severance and ad valorem taxes included in this month’s net profits calculation were $0.06 million, an increase of approximately $0.01 million from the prior month’s distribution period. T2S informed the Trust that this increase was primarily due to increases in severance taxes and ad valorem taxes.

 

 

Capital expenditures were $41,794 for the current month. T2S informed the Trust that this amount primarily reflected tangible drilling and tangible completion costs.

T2S informed the Trust that this month’s net profits calculation included a net holdback of $165,000 (net $132,000 to the Trust), consisting of a $62,500 holdback (net $50,000 to the Trust) for future ad valorem taxes and a $150,000 holdback (net $120,000 to the Trust) for future plugging and abandonment costs, partially offset by a $47,500 partial release (net $38,000 to the Trust) of funds previously held back for workover expenses.

 

As previously disclosed in the Trust’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, T2S will recoup a prior overpayment to the Trust of $350,812.13, without interest, in six monthly installments beginning with the November 2026 distribution. This month's net profits calculation reflects the first installment, which reduced oil sales volumes by 1,185 Bbls (net 948 Bbls to the Trust) and oil sales revenue by $76,618 (net $61,294 to the Trust), less $3,532 of related severance taxes (net $2,826 to the Trust), for a total reduction of $58,469 to the Trust.

 

About PermRock Royalty Trust

PermRock Royalty Trust is a Delaware statutory trust formed to own a net profits interest representing the right to receive 80% of the net profits from the sale of oil and natural gas production from certain properties owned and operated by T2S in the Permian Basin of West Texas. For more information on PermRock Royalty Trust, please visit our website at www.permrock.com.
