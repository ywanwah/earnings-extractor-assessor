Gross margin was 61.7% in the second quarter of fiscal 2027, compared with 59.5% in the second quarter of fiscal 2026. The primary drivers of the increase in the gross margin percentage included tariffs refunded, the continued transformation of the product portfolio to higher-margin offerings and the absence of amortization of fair value inventory step-up. Operating expenses were $145.8 million, up $9.5 million, or 7.0%, compared with the second quarter of fiscal 2026. The increase in operating expenses was driven by higher personnel-related costs and freight charges. The Company had operating income of $75.1 million and a 21.0% operating margin in the second quarter of fiscal 2027, compared with operating income of $58.5 million and a 17.9% operating margin in the second quarter of fiscal 2026. The effective income tax rate was 26.3% in the second quarter of fiscal 2027, compared with 24.6% in the second quarter of fiscal 2026. The increase in the effective income tax rate was primarily due to valuation allowances recorded against deferred tax assets in jurisdictions with losses from recent acquisitions. Second quarter fiscal 2027 net income and earnings per diluted share were $46.3 million and $1.00, respectively, compared with $38.7 million and $0.81, respectively, in the second quarter of fiscal 2026.

Second quarter fiscal 2027 adjusted gross margin was 61.9%, up 140 basis points compared with the prior year period. The primary drivers of the increase in the adjusted gross margin percentage included tariffs refunded and the continued transformation of the product portfolio to higher-margin offerings, partially offset by foreign currency fluctuations.
Adjusted operating expenses were $127.6 million, up $16.8 million, or 15.2%, compared with the second quarter of fiscal 2026. The increase in adjusted operating expenses was driven by higher personnel-related costs and freight charges. Adjusted operating income for the second quarter of fiscal 2027 was $94.1 million, up $6.8 million, or 7.8%, compared with the second quarter of fiscal 2026. Adjusted operating margin was 26.3%, down 40 basis points when compared with the same period of fiscal 2026. The adjusted income tax rate for the second quarter of fiscal 2027 was 24.7%, which was flat when compared with 24.7% in the second quarter of fiscal 2026.
Second quarter fiscal 2027 adjusted net income was $62.6 million, up $2.3 million, or 3.8%, and adjusted earnings per diluted share was $1.35, up 6.3%, each when compared with the same period of fiscal 2026.
BALANCE SHEET AND CASH FLOW
Cash on hand as of September 26, 2026 was $260.7 million, an increase of $15.3 million since the end of fiscal 2026, primarily driven by cash flow provided from operating activities, partially offset by cash outflows for the repayment of revolving credit facility borrowings, capital expenditures and contingent consideration payments.

Second quarter fiscal 2027 cash flow from operating activities was $128.1 million, up $16.8 million, and free cash flow was $103.8 million, up $15.1 million, each when compared with the same period of fiscal 2026. The primary driver of increased operating cash flow as compared to the same period of fiscal 2026 was favorable working capital adjustments driven by the timing of payments made and cash collections. Free cash flow was impacted by higher capital expenditures, which was partially offset by lower non-cash transfers from Haemonetics equipment.

ABOUT HAEMONETICS
Haemonetics is a global medical technology company dedicated to improving the quality, effectiveness and efficiency of health care. Our Apheresis business features proprietary technologies designed to enhance safety, yield, donor satisfaction and operational efficiency for plasma and blood collectors around the world. Our MedSurg business offers Blood Management Technologies to help inform treatment decisions and optimize the management of blood products, and Interventional Technologies, including advanced vascular closure systems and sensor-guided technologies, designed to drive procedural effectiveness and elevate the patient experience. To learn more about Haemonetics, visit www.haemonetics.com.
