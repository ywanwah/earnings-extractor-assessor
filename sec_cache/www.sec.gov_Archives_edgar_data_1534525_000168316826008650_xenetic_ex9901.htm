Xenetic Biosciences Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Highlights Strategic Business Updates

 

Proposed share exchange transaction expected to create a multi-indication clinical stage company focused on targeting neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs)

 

Joint pipeline includes four first-in-class programs, including pivotal-stage programs in sepsis and systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE), both of which have received FDA Breakthrough Device Designation

 

Ongoing generation of translational data demonstrating that DNase I enhanced antitumor activity across four CAR targets in preclinical models of leukemia, lymphoma, multiple myeloma and glioblastoma

 

First clinical evaluation of Xenetic’s systemic DNase technology combined with CAR T-cell therapy in an investigator-initiated study conducted at Tel Aviv Sourasky University Medical Center with initial efficacy data expected in Q2 2027

 

FRAMINGHAM, MA – (November 13, 2026) – Xenetic Biosciences, Inc. (NASDAQ: XBIO) (“Xenetic” or the “Company”), a biopharmaceutical company focused on advancing innovative immuno-oncology technologies addressing difficult to treat cancers, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and highlighted its proposed share exchange transaction with Santersus AG ("Santersus"), which is expected to establish a Nasdaq-listed, clinical-stage company with a differentiated pipeline targeting neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs) across multiple high-value therapeutic indications.

 

"This is a defining period for Xenetic as we work toward completing a transaction that we believe has the potential to fundamentally transform the Company's clinical development profile and long-term value proposition," said James Parslow, Interim Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer of Xenetic Biosciences. "Santersus is expected to bring an established clinical-stage platform, two FDA Breakthrough Device Designations and a pipeline targeting serious diseases with substantial unmet medical needs. Importantly, the proposed combined company would have multiple development programs and potential catalysts, including an ongoing pivotal sepsis study, planned advancement in lupus and an anticipated oncology clinical readout in 2027."

 

Mr. Parslow continued, "We believe the complementary nature of NucleoCapture® and Xenetic's DNase platform provides a differentiated opportunity to address NETs through distinct mechanisms, potentially expanding the therapeutic reach of the combined platform. Our focus remains on advancing the proposed transaction and positioning the combined organization to execute against its development priorities and build sustainable stockholder value."

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Strategic Highlights

 

·Initiated Clinical Evaluation of DNase I in Combination with CAR T-Cell Therapy: Announced the first patient dosed in an investigator-initiated, Phase I (NCT07361224) clinical study evaluating systemic recombinant human DNase I in combination with anti-CD19 CAR T-cell therapy in patients with large B-cell lymphoma (LBCL). The study is being conducted at Tel Aviv Sourasky University Medical Center in Israel in collaboration with PeriNess Ltd., with initial efficacy data expected in the second quarter of 2027.
·Advanced Pre-Clinical Development Strategy in CAR T cell therapy: Research examines a self-reinforcing niche of therapy resistance created by extracellular chromatin and NETs released during CAR T-cell killing and its reversal by DNase I.
·Announced Definitive Exchange Agreement with Santersus AG: Entered into a definitive share exchange agreement to, subject to Xenetic’s stockholder approval, acquire all of the outstanding share capital of Santersus AG in exchange for newly issued shares of Xenetic in an all-stock transaction (the “Proposed Transaction”). The Proposed Transaction is expected to create a Nasdaq-listed clinical-stage company focused on targeting neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs) across multiple therapeutic indications. After closing, the combined company is expected to continue to trade on the Nasdaq Capital Market as Santersus Bio, Inc., under new ticker symbol “SNTS”. The Proposed Transaction has been unanimously approved by the Board of Directors of Santersus. Xenetic’s Board of Directors – acting on the unanimous recommendation of its independent Special Committee – has also approved the Proposed Transaction and recommends that Xenetic stockholders vote in favor of the Proposed Transaction. The Proposed Transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to approval by Xenetic stockholders, the shares of Xenetic common stock issuable in the Proposed Transaction having been approved for listing on Nasdaq, the effectiveness of a resale registration statement on Form S-1 to be filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”), and the satisfaction or waiver of other customary closing conditions.

 

Summary of Financial Results for Third Quarter 2026

 

Royalty revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 decreased approximately 29% to approximately $0.7 million, compared to approximately $1.0 million for the comparable prior-year period. The decrease was primarily attributable to positive royalty adjustments recognized from certain countries during the third quarter of 2025 under the Company's sublicense agreement with Takeda Pharmaceutical Co. Ltd.

 

Research and development expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 decreased approximately 31% to approximately $0.5 million, compared to approximately $0.8 million for the comparable prior-year period, primarily due to lower manufacturing development costs.

 

General and administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 increased approximately 138% to approximately $1.9 million, compared to approximately $0.8 million for the comparable quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to higher legal and financial advisory expenses associated with the Proposed Transaction.

 

Net loss for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $1.7 million, or $0.72 per basic and diluted share, compared to approximately $0.5 million, or $0.33 per basic and diluted share, for the same period in 2025.

 

Cash and cash equivalents totaled approximately $5.8 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to approximately $7.9 million as of December 31, 2025.

 

About Xenetic Biosciences

 

Xenetic Biosciences, Inc. is a biopharmaceutical company focused on advancing innovative immuno-oncology technologies addressing difficult to treat cancers. The Company's proprietary DNase technology is designed to improve outcomes of existing treatments, including immunotherapies, by targeting neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs), which are involved in cancer progression. Xenetic is currently focused on advancing its systemic DNase program into the clinic as an adjunctive therapy for pancreatic carcinoma and locally advanced or metastatic solid tumors.


For more information, please visit the Company's website at www.xeneticbio.com and connect on X, LinkedIn, and Facebook.
