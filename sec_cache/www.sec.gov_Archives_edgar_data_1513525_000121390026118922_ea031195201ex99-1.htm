Azora Therapeutics Reports 2026 Third Quarter Financial Results
and Provides Business Update

 

Azora Therapeutics commenced trading on Nasdaq as “AZR”

 

New executive team and Board of Directors appointed

 

AT177 IND submission and Phase 1a initiation on track for the first half of 2027

 

Los Angeles, CA, November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Azora Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: AZR) (“Azora” or the “Company”), a biopharmaceutical company developing locally acting treatments for serious immune-mediated and inflammatory diseases, today provided a business update and reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

During the quarter, Azora continued to advance AT177 through IND-enabling studies and expanded its scientific and operating team. AT177 is a proprietary, oral, colon-targeted aryl-hydrocarbon receptor (AhR) agonist designed to restore mucosal immune homeostasis at the site of disease with minimal systemic exposure.

 

“With our transition to Nasdaq, Azora is well positioned for success to bring AhR biology to people living with ulcerative colitis,” said Matt Davidson, Ph.D., President, Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer. “We are pleased to have recently strengthened our leadership team, appointing Celia Economides as CFO and David Rubenstein, M.D., Ph.D., as Chief Scientist. Dr. Rubenstein led the development and FDA approval of the first AhR agonist. We have further added to our depth of experience through the appointment of new Board members who bring inflammatory disease, clinical development and company building expertise to Azora. Our focus now is execution excellence, and we remain on track to submit an IND for AT177 and initiate our Phase 1a trial in the first half of 2027.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Cash position

 

Cash and cash equivalents were $22.0 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $28.7 million as of June 30, 2026. Existing cash and cash equivalents are anticipated to fund the Company’s current operating plan, including completion of IND-enabling studies and the Phase 1a trial. This does not assume any proceeds from the issuance and sale of milestone warrants.

Research and development (R&D) expenses increased by approximately $1.3 million to $1.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $0.5 million for the same period in 2025. The increase reflects preclinical and toxicology work for AT177.

The third quarter net loss includes a non-cash charge of $57.1 million for the change in fair value of the milestone warrant rights liability, recorded before those rights were reclassified to equity.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had a stockholders’ deficit of $21.1 million, primarily because $38.5 million of Series A convertible preferred stock was classified as temporary equity pending conversion. On October 28, 2026, the stockholders approved the conversion of the Series A convertible preferred stock, and as a result the Company expects to reclassify the $38.5 million carrying value of the Series A convertible preferred stock into stockholders’ equity in the fourth quarter of 2026. On November 2, 2026, 9,924.260 shares of Series A preferred stock converted into 9,924,260 shares of common stock.

 

About AT177

 

AT177 is a fully synthetic, patented, oral AhR agonist designed to restore mucosal immune homeostasis at the site of disease with minimal systemic exposure. AT177 is a prodrug of the active ingredient, indirubin, a potent AhR agonist derived from indigo naturalis, a botanical extract that has been evaluated in clinical studies in patients with ulcerative colitis but has not been approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. AT177’s colon-targeted formulation is designed to deliver therapeutic AhR engagement directly to the colonic mucosa with the goal of minimizing the systemic AhR exposure associated with adverse effects. In preclinical studies, AT177 demonstrated robust local colonic AhR activation with markedly limited systemic exposure. AT177 is currently in IND-enabling studies, with an IND submission and Phase 1a clinical trial planned for the first half of 2027.

 

About Azora Therapeutics

 

Azora Therapeutics is a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing treatments for serious inflammatory diseases. The company was launched out of Stanford University after being incubated in the translational medicine SPARK program. Azora owns worldwide royalty-free rights to its technology, which is based on the development of oral agonists of the aryl hydrocarbon receptor. Azora’s lead program is AT177, a proprietary colon-targeted aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AhR) agonist designed to enable localized activation with limited systemic exposure in development for ulcerative colitis. More information on Azora can be found on the company’s website at www.AzoraTherapeutics.com.
