Silvercrest Asset Management Group Inc. Reports Q3 2026 Results

New York, NY – October 29, 2026 - Silvercrest Asset Management Group Inc. (NASDAQ: SAMG) (the “Company” or “Silvercrest”) today reported the results of its operations for the quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

Business Update

Silvercrest continued its strategic initiatives during the third quarter, and we continue to progress with the plan we have described over the past two years. Discretionary assets under management (“AUM”) which drives the firm’s revenue decreased $939.2 million during the third quarter to $23.8 billion, primarily due to weaker markets. Of that, $753.1 million had a revenue effect. The third quarter’s weaker markets followed unusually strong second quarter markets, with the result that capital growth has contributed $1.7 billion in AUM this year to date. Net flows for the quarter were essentially flat at $58.3 million. Silvercrest added $182.2 million in organic new client accounts, which was the best quarter during 2026, and our net flows included an affirming $373.0 million follow-on investment in the firm’s global value equity strategy. Offsetting outflows during the quarter were primarily attributable to seasonal high net worth client withdrawals for tax payments as well as some institutional outflows.

While total AUM stands at $36.2 billion, we have discussed in prior quarters that non-discretionary AUM are associated with a small portion of revenue, as most of non-discretionary AUM represents flat fee family office and related services. Therefore, changes in the level of non-discretionary AUM do not correlate to revenue change. We will adjust how the firm reports non-discretionary at the end of the first quarter of 2027, likely eliminating the non-discretionary AUM category. This adjustment will substantially lower reported non-discretionary and total AUM on a one-time basis without revenue effect. We seek to provide investors with a clear picture of the discretionary AUM and the flows and economics that drive our business.

Year to date, revenue increased 2% year over year, reflecting a recovery in average AUM levels weighed down by first-quarter outflows. Our estimated annual run rate increased by a similar percentage year over year, since September 30, 2025.

We announced during the third quarter that we received our full MiFID license through the Central Bank of Ireland. We also opened our Dublin office to serve both high net worth and institutional clients in Europe. With the completion of our Atlanta and Dublin offices, our UCITS fund, Australian trust, and regulatory licensing effort, we do expect administrative and legal costs associated with these initiatives to decline and begin contributing revenue.

Our institutional pipeline has continued to grow substantially and remains robust, particularly in our Global and International Equity strategies, which continue to deliver exceptional performance. Our pipeline quality has also improved with both more possible AUM as well as in number of opportunities. We completed progress on building our global infrastructure and distribution for our current revenue push. We also have achieved important third-party ratings for our strategies and vehicles, and we continue to seek additional ratings with major global consultants to further open institutional distribution channels.

As previously discussed, Silvercrest will continue to adjust our compensation ratio to match compelling opportunities to organically grow the business. Total compensation and benefits expense was $62.9 million, representing 66.3% of revenue, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. As we have consistently communicated, our earnings and Adjusted EBITDA¹ reflect the deliberate cost of the most significant investment program in the firm's history, and we expect the compensation ratio to remain elevated as these investments mature.

Also, we have discussed with and reminded investors, shareholders approved of an increase in the number of shares issuable under our equity incentive plan. Intellectual capital is Silvercrest's most important resource, and we have made substantial investments in excellent talent. We intend to imminently make equity grants to the professionals who are building our business and executing our strategy. Attracting and motivating our professionals and working to align their long-term interests with those of our shareholders is fundamental to how we intend to grow the firm and compound value through this investment cycle and beyond.

Third Quarter 2026 vs. Third Quarter 2025

Revenue increased by $1.4 million, or 4.5%, to $32.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, from $31.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. This increase was mainly due to an increase in the average annual management fee.

Total expenses increased by $0.8 million, or 2.9%, to $30.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, from $30.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Compensation and benefits expense decreased by $0.5 million, or 2.1%, to $21.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, from $21.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily attributable to decreases in the accrual for bonuses of $0.9 million and severance of $0.1 million, partially offset by increases in salaries and benefits of $0.2 million primarily as a result of merit-based increases and newly-hired staff, including our new staff in Ireland and in equity-based compensation of $0.3 million. General and administrative expenses increased by $1.3 million, or 16.0%, to $9.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $8.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. This was primarily attributable to increases in professional fees of $0.1 million, occupancy and related costs of $0.1 million primarily related to new office space in Singapore, travel and entertainment expenses of $0.5 million, portfolio and systems expense of $0.4 million, charitable donations of $0.1 million, business taxes of $0.1 million and depreciation and amortization of $0.1 million, partially offset by a decrease in recruiting costs of $0.1 million.

Consolidated net income was $1.2 million, or 3.7% of revenue, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to consolidated net income of $1.1 million, or 3.5% of revenue, for the same period in the prior year. Net income attributable to Silvercrest was $0.6 million, or $0.08 per basic and diluted share, for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Our adjusted net income1 was $1.9 million, or $0.16 per adjusted basic and adjusted diluted share2 for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

Adjusted EBITDA1 was $4.3 million, or 13.5% of revenue, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $4.5 million, or 14.5% of revenue, for the same period in the prior year.

Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 vs. Nine Months Ended September 30, 2025

Revenue increased by $1.5 million, or 1.6%, to $94.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, from $93.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. This increase was mainly due to an increase in the average annual management fee.

Total expenses increased by $7.6 million, or 9.2%, to $90.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, from $83.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Compensation and benefits expense increased by $3.5 million, or 5.9%, to $62.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, from $59.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to increases in salaries and benefits of $1.2 million primarily as a result of merit-based increases and newly-hired staff, including our new staff in Ireland, and in the accrual for bonuses of $1.5 million, equity-based compensation of $0.5 million and severance of $0.3 million. General and administrative expenses increased by $4.1 million, or 17.2%, to $27.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, from $23.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. This was primarily attributable to increases in professional fees of $1.7 million, occupancy and related costs of $0.4 million primarily related to new office space in Singapore, travel and entertainment expenses of $1.0 million, portfolio and systems expense of $0.9 million, business taxes of $0.1 million and depreciation and amortization of $0.2 million, partially offset by a decrease in recruiting costs of $0.2 million.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

To provide investors with additional insight, promote transparency and allow for a more comprehensive understanding of the information used by management in its financial and operational decision-making, we supplement our consolidated financial statements presented on a basis consistent with GAAP with Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Earnings Per Share, which are non-GAAP financial measures of earnings. These adjustments, and the non-GAAP financial measures that are derived from them, provide supplemental information to analyze our operations between periods and over time. Investors should consider our non-GAAP financial measures in addition to, and not as a substitute for, financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP.

•
EBITDA represents net income before provision for income taxes, interest income, interest expense, depreciation and amortization.
•
We define Adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA without giving effect to the Delaware franchise tax, equity-based compensation, professional fees associated with acquisitions or financing transactions, gains on extinguishment of debt or other obligations related to acquisitions, impairment charges and losses on disposals or abandonment of assets and leaseholds, client reimbursements and fund redemption costs, severance and other similar expenses. We believe that it is important to management and investors to supplement our consolidated financial statements presented on a GAAP basis with Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure of earnings, as this measure provides a perspective of recurring earnings of the Company, taking into account earnings attributable to both Class A and Class B stockholders.
•
Adjusted EBITDA Margin is calculated by dividing Adjusted EBITDA by total revenue. We believe that it is important to management and investors to supplement our consolidated financial statements presented on a GAAP basis with Adjusted EBITDA Margin, a non-GAAP financial measure of earnings, as this measure provides a perspective of recurring profitability of the Company, taking into account profitability attributable to both Class A and Class B stockholders.
•
Adjusted Net Income represents recurring net income without giving effect to the Delaware franchise tax, professional fees associated with acquisitions or financing transactions, losses on forgiveness of notes receivable from our partners, gains on extinguishment of debt or other obligations related to acquisitions, impairment charges and losses on disposals or abandonment of assets and leaseholds, client reimbursements and fund redemption costs, severance and other similar expenses. Furthermore, Adjusted Net Income includes income tax expense assuming a blended corporate rate of 26%. We believe that it is important to management and investors to supplement our consolidated financial statements presented on a GAAP basis with Adjusted Net Income, a non-GAAP financial measure of earnings, as this measure provides a perspective of recurring income of the Company, taking into account income attributable to both Class A and Class B stockholders.
•
Adjusted Earnings Per Share represents Adjusted Net Income divided by the actual Class A and Class B shares outstanding as of the end of the reporting period for basic Adjusted Earnings Per Share, and to the extent dilutive, we add unvested restricted stock units and non-qualified stock options to the total shares outstanding to compute diluted Adjusted Earnings Per Share. As a result of our structure, which includes a non-controlling interest, we believe that it is important to management and investors to supplement our consolidated financial statements presented on a GAAP basis with Adjusted Earnings Per Share, a non-GAAP financial measure of earnings, as this measure provides a perspective of recurring earnings per share of the Company as a whole as opposed to being limited to our Class A common stock.

The market indices used to compare to the performance of Silvercrest’s strategies are as follows:

 

The Russell 1000 Index is a capitalization-weighted, unmanaged index that measures the 1000 largest companies in the Russell 3000. The Russell 1000 Value Index is a capitalization-weighted, unmanaged index that includes those Russell 1000 Index companies with lower price-to-book ratios and lower expected growth values.

 

The Russell 2000 Index is a capitalization-weighted, unmanaged index that measures the 2000 smallest companies in the Russell 3000. The Russell 2000 Value Index is a capitalization-weighted, unmanaged index that includes those Russell 2000 Index companies with lower price-to-book ratios and lower expected growth values. The Russell 2000 Growth Index is a capitalization-weighted, unmanaged index that includes those Russell 2000 Index companies with higher price-to-book ratios and higher forecasted growth.

 

The Russell 2500 Index is a capitalization-weighted, unmanaged index that measures the 2500 smallest companies in the Russell 3000. The Russell 2500 Value Index is a capitalization-weighted, unmanaged index that includes those Russell 2500 Index companies with lower price-to-book ratios and lower expected growth values. The Russell 2500 Growth Index is a capitalization-weighted, unmanaged index that includes those Russell 2500 Index companies with higher price-to-book ratios and higher forecasted growth.
