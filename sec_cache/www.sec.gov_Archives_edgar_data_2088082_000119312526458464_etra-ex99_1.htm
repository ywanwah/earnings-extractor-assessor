Electra Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Recent Portfolio Highlights

 

Ipsoprubart Advancing in Global Registrational Program as Potential First Treatment for sHLH Broadly; Enrollment Completion Expected in Second Half of 2027

 

Ipsoprubart Therapeutic Expansion Underway in Phase 1 Trial for T/NK Cell Malignancies, with Initial Data Expected in the Second Half of 2027

 

ELA822 Healthy Volunteer Data Expected in the First Half of 2027, with Patient Trial Initiation Planned for Mid-2027

 

Completed $350 Million IPO to Fund Advancement of SIRP-Targeting Portfolio

 

SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, Calif., Nov. 12, 2026 -- Electra Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: ETRA), a late clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on pioneering a new class of precision medicines for the treatment of immune-mediated diseases and cancer, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provided an overview of its portfolio and recent business highlights.

“Our recent IPO positions Electra to build on our first-mover advantage in SIRP-targeted precision immune cell depletion,” said Kathy Dong, PharmD, MBA, President and Chief Executive Officer of Electra Therapeutics. “Our deep expertise in SIRP biology and proprietary library of SIRP-targeting antibodies enable us to selectively address distinct pathogenic immune cell populations, creating therapeutic optionality across immune-mediated diseases and cancer. Building on compelling clinical data in sHLH, we are advancing ipsoprubart through a global registrational program and evaluating its potential in T/NK cell malignancies. With ELA822 now in Phase 1, we are advancing multiple programs with the potential to benefit patients and create value for our stakeholders. We anticipate several important milestones in 2027, including ELA822 healthy volunteer data expected in the first half of the year, followed by ipsoprubart data in T/NK cell malignancies and completion of enrollment in the sHLH registrational program in the second half.”

Electra’s novel approach targets signal regulatory proteins (SIRP), a family of cell surface receptors whose expression increases upon activation in specific immune cell populations. This approach is designed to selectively deplete disease-driving immune cells while preserving normal immune function. By offering a more targeted alternative to broad immunosuppression, this approach has the potential to bring greater precision to the treatment for immune-mediated diseases, much as precision oncology has transformed cancer care.

 

Portfolio Overview

•
Ipsoprubart Global Registrational Development for sHLH: The company’s lead product candidate, ipsoprubart, is a novel pan-SIRP monoclonal antibody in development for secondary hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis (sHLH), designed to selectively deplete the pathogenic SIRPα/b1-positive myeloid cells and SIRPg-positive T cells characteristic of sHLH.

 

o
In a Phase 1b trial, ipsoprubart was generally well tolerated and demonstrated 100% overall survival at eight weeks and a 100% overall response rate at the week four assessment in 12 frontline patients with malignancy-associated HLH, the largest subset of sHLH and the population with the poorest outcomes.
o
The company’s global registrational program comprises SURPASS, a Phase 2/3 trial in newly diagnosed, treatment-naïve sHLH patients, and COMPASS, a retrospective natural history study designed to provide an external control comparator, with enrollment completion expected in the second half of 2027.

Ipsoprubart for T Cell Malignancies: Ipsoprubart is also being evaluated as single-agent therapy in patients with relapsed/refractory T/NK cell malignancies.
o
Among eight sHLH patients with underlying T- or B-cell lymphomas and available objective tumor response assessments across the Phase 1b trial and Expanded Access Program, all eight patients had an objective tumor response and seven had complete responses.
o
Based on this anti-tumor activity, the company is conducting an ongoing Phase 1 trial in patients with relapsed/refractory T/NK malignancies, with initial data expected in the second half of 2027.

 

•
ELA822 for Chronic Immune and Inflammatory Diseases: ELA822 is a novel SIRPγ-specific antibody in development for chronic T cell-mediated immune and inflammatory diseases, designed to selectively deplete activated, pathogenic T cells while sparing naïve and regulatory T cells.

 

o
In nonclinical models of giant cell arteritis and graft-versus-host disease, ELA822 reduced cytotoxic T cell infiltration into tissues and inflammatory cytokine production, with improvements in disease activity and survival.
o
In September 2026, Electra initiated a randomized, placebo-controlled Phase 1 trial of ELA822 in healthy volunteers, with data expected in the first half of 2027. The company plans to initiate a first-in-disease trial in patients with T cell-mediated immune disorders in mid-2027, subject to regulatory approval.

About Electra Therapeutics

Electra Therapeutics is a late clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on pioneering a new class of precision medicines for the treatment of immune-mediated diseases and cancer. The company’s novel approach targets signal regulatory proteins (SIRP), a family of cell surface receptors whose expression is restricted to specific immune cell populations and increases upon activation, to enable selective depletion of disease-driving cells while preserving normal immune function. The company’s lead product candidate, ipsoprubart (ELA026), is a novel pan-SIRP monoclonal antibody currently in a global registrational program for secondary hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis (sHLH), a severe hyperinflammatory syndrome for which there are no broadly approved therapies and survival has remained consistently poor over the past two decades. Ipsoprubart is also being evaluated in a Phase 1 clinical trial in patients with relapsed/refractory T/NK cell malignancies. ELA822, the company’s second clinical-stage product candidate, is a novel SIRPγ-specific antibody being developed for a broad range of chronic T cell-mediated immune and inflammatory diseases. For more information, please visit electra-therapeutics.com and follow us on LinkedIn.
