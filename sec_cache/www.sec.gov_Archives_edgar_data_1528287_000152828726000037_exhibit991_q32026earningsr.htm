NeuroPace Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Revised Full Year Guidance

Q3 2026 total revenue of $23.1 million, including $22.9 million in RNS revenue representing 1% growth

Revises full year 2026 revenue guidance to $91 million to $92 million; focused on improving commercial execution and accelerating growth

Advances innovation pipeline with NeuroPace Remote Care™ FDA submission and upcoming IGE PMA-S Amendment, supporting significant long-term growth opportunities

Mountain View, Calif. – November 12, 2026 – NeuroPace, Inc. (Nasdaq: NPCE), a medical device company focused on transforming the lives of people living with epilepsy, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Total revenue of $23.1 million in the third quarter of 2026
•RNS System revenue of $22.9 million in the quarter, representing 1.4% growth compared to the third quarter of 2025
•Net loss in the third quarter of 2026 was ($3.3) million compared to ($4.9) million in the third quarter of 2025
•Adjusted EBITDA of $0.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to ($1.3) million in the third quarter of 2025

Third Quarter 2026 Operational & Strategic Highlights
•Completed a Submission Issue Request (SIR) meeting with the FDA in October regarding the Company’s premarket approval (PMA) supplement for idiopathic generalized epilepsy (IGE). NeuroPace is incorporating the agency’s feedback into an amendment and expects to submit the amendment around year end.
•Submitted a PMA-Supplement for Remote Care to the FDA, with a planned 180-day review cycle. Remote Care is designed to enable physicians to program the RNS System through telehealth, reducing the need for in-person visits and helping address travel and geographic barriers that can limit access to RNS therapy, particularly for patients who live farther from epilepsy centers.
•Initiated enrollment in AURORA, a real-world evidence study designed to support expansion of the RNS System’s indication to adolescents aged 12–17 with drug-resistant focal epilepsy. Data collection is expected to be completed in early 2027, followed by analysis and submission to the FDA.

“Third-quarter revenue was below our expectations, and we have taken and are taking action to improve commercial execution and return the business to historical growth rates,” said Joel Becker, President and Chief Executive Officer of NeuroPace. “The shortfall was concentrated in several of our largest accounts and reflected isolated account-level issues and changes to our field organization that created more disruption than anticipated. We are addressing these factors, and fourth-quarter scheduled cases have begun to recover. Ongoing demand for RNS therapy, compelling clinical outcomes and the substantial opportunity within our current indication all remain and reinforce our confidence in the business performing in line with historical growth rates. Our focus is on continuing to translate that opportunity into consistent growth through deeper customer engagement and stronger commercial execution.”

“Our innovation pipeline represents one of the most significant opportunities ahead for NeuroPace, combining potential indication expansion with capabilities that can make RNS therapy more accessible, efficient and personalized,” Becker continued. “Following our SIR meeting with the FDA, we are preparing the amendment


to our IGE submission. We have also initiated enrollment in AURORA, our real-world evidence study supporting potential expansion into adolescent focal epilepsy, and submitted Remote Care for FDA review. Together with ECoG Assistant, our broader AI development programs and our next-generation platform, these initiatives can extend our reach to new patient populations and strengthen the differentiated value of RNS therapy. We believe the opportunity in our core business and the breadth of our pipeline position NeuroPace for substantial long-term growth.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
RNS System revenue totaled $22.9 million in the third quarter of 2026, representing growth of 1.4% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Total revenue in the third quarter of 2026 declined 1.1% to $23.1 million, compared with revenue of $23.4 million in the third quarter of 2025.

Non-GAAP gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 84.8%, compared with 82.8% in the third quarter of 2025. The slight year-over-year growth was driven by improved labor absorption. Total GAAP gross margin from continuing operations in the third quarter of 2026 was 84.3%.

Non-GAAP operating expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were $19.2 million, compared with $20.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. GAAP operating expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were $21.6 million.

Non-GAAP sales and marketing expense in the third quarter of 2026 was $10.5 million, compared with $11.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year decline was largely due to a decrease in personnel-related expenses resulting from a decrease in sales and field support personnel costs, including sales commissions, partially offset by an increase in headcount.

Non-GAAP research and development expense in the third quarter of 2026 was $5.5 million, compared with $5.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year decline was primarily driven by a decrease in personnel-related expenses, including variable compensation, partially offset by less grant funds received primarily from the National Institutes of Health which are recognized as a reduction in research and development expenses.

Non-GAAP general and administrative expense in the third quarter of 2026 was $3.2 million compared with $3.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year decline was largely due to a decrease in personnel-related expenses from variable compensation, partially offset by an increase from headcount increase.

Non-GAAP income from operations was $0.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with loss from operations of ($1.4) million in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net loss was ($0.9) million for the third quarter of 2026 compared with net loss of ($2.3) million in the third quarter of 2025. GAAP net loss in the third quarter of 2026 was ($3.3) million.

The Company’s cash, cash equivalents, short-term investments and restricted cash balance as of September 30, 2026 was $49.5 million compared with $51.9 million at the end of the prior quarter. Long-term borrowings totaled $59.2 million as of September 30, 2026.     

Full Year 2026 Financial Guidance
•Revise total revenue guidance for full year 2026 to between $91 million and $92 million, representing underlying RNS growth of 10% to 11%. This compares to previously issued guidance of $99.5 million to $101.5 million. This guidance continues to reflect expected service revenue of approximately $1 million.
•Increase full year non-GAAP gross margin to between 83.0% and 83.5%, an increase compared to 82.0% to 83.0% previously.
•Revise full year non-GAAP operating expenses to be between $85 million and $86 million, compared to previous guidance of $90 million to $92 million. This revised range continues to exclude approximately $10 million in stock-based compensation, a non-cash expense.


•Revise Adjusted EBITDA to be between ($8.5) million and ($9.5) million compared to previous guidance between ($7.5) million to ($8.5) million.

About NeuroPace, Inc.
Based in Mountain View, Calif., NeuroPace is a medical device company focused on transforming the lives of people living with epilepsy by reducing or eliminating the occurrence of debilitating seizures. Its novel and differentiated RNS System is the first and only commercially available, brain-responsive platform that delivers personalized, real-time treatment at the seizure source. This platform can drive a better standard of care for patients living with drug-resistant epilepsy and has the potential to offer a more personalized solution and improved outcomes to the large population of patients suffering from other brain disorders.
