PITTSBURGH, PA, October 29, 2026 – Howmet Aerospace (NYSE: HWM) announced results today for the third quarter 2026.

 

Howmet Aerospace Executive Chairman and Chief Executive Officer John Plant said, “Howmet's performance was solid in all dimensions, with third quarter results exceeding the high end of guidance. Revenue grew 29% year over year and 25% organically, the strongest quarterly growth rate on record for Howmet. Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 310 basis points year over year to 32.5%. Free cash flow was a robust $562 million after $147 million in capital expenditures, as we continue to invest for growth. The free cash flow supported $500 million in common stock repurchases in the third quarter, lifting year-to-date repurchases to $1.1 billion. The Board of Directors authorized an increase in the share repurchase program by $5 billion to $5.397 billion, reflecting confidence in the Company's strong free cash flow outlook.”

 

Mr. Plant continued, “Our trajectory points to a healthy fourth quarter and the conclusion of another strong year for Howmet. The commercial aerospace market continues to look robust, underpinned by the extraordinary aircraft order backlog that supports expected production volume increases throughout 2027 and beyond. We do not expect any material impact on our spares demand from the Middle East conflict given the backlog of engine shop visits and requirements to support next generation narrowbody engines. Defense markets remain solid, with further spares needs and likely increases in legacy fighter builds. Of particular note was the 45% year-over-year gas turbines revenue growth, which demonstrates both the extraordinary addition to output and, even more importantly, the increase in market share. Demand in the gas turbines market continues to strengthen with customers looking to increase volumes beyond previous expectations, which supports the outlook beyond 2028. Our capital expenditure plans envisage elevated spend beyond 2027 to support growth in both aerospace and defense as well as gas turbines. Our disciplined focus on free cash flow conversion will remain steadfast throughout this elevated investment period. The commercial transportation market continues to experience recovery in North America. Looking forward to 2027, we envisage revenue of approximately $11.5 billion, representing approximately 12% growth from the increased 2026 guidance.”

Consolidated Results

 

Howmet Aerospace reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $2.70 billion, up 29% year over year with organic growth of 25%, and Adjusted EPS of $1.46, up 54% year over year. Revenue was driven by 32% growth in the commercial aerospace market, 10% growth in the defense aerospace market, 45% growth in the gas turbines market, and 28% growth in the commercial transportation market.

 

The Company reported Adjusted EBITDA of $879 million, up 43% year over year. The year-over-year increase was driven by growth in the commercial aerospace, defense aerospace, gas turbines, and commercial transportation markets. Adjusted EBITDA margin was up approximately 310 basis points year over year at 32.5%.

Repurchased $500 Million of Common Stock in Third Quarter 2026; $1.1B Year To Date

Board of Directors Approved a $5 Billion Increase to the Share Repurchase Program

 

On October 28, 2026 the Board of Directors authorized an increase in the Company's share repurchase program by $5 billion, which, together with the remaining authorization of $397 million, results in authorization to repurchase up to $5.397 billion of the Company's outstanding stock.

 

Paid Down $60 Million of Debt

 

In the third quarter 2026, the Company paid down $60 million of borrowings on its commercial paper program. At the end of the quarter, $390 million of the $1 billion commercial paper program was outstanding.

 

Quarterly Common Stock Dividend Increases 17% to $0.14 Per Share in Third Quarter 2026

 

On July 27, 2026, the Board of Directors declared a dividend of $0.14 per share on its common stock, which was paid on August 25, 2026. The quarterly dividend represents a 17% increase from the second quarter 2026 dividend of $0.12 per share.

About Howmet Aerospace

 

Howmet Aerospace Inc., headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, is a leading global provider of advanced engineered solutions for the aerospace, gas turbine, and transportation industries. The Company’s primary businesses focus on engine components, fastening systems, and airframe structural components necessary for mission-critical performance and efficiency, including in aerospace, defense, and gas turbine applications, as well as forged aluminum wheels for commercial transportation. With approximately 1,200 granted and pending patents, the Company’s differentiated technologies enable lighter, more fuel-efficient aircraft and commercial trucks to operate with a lower carbon footprint. For more information, visit www.howmet.com.
