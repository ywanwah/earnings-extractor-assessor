Phathom Pharmaceuticals Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

 

  •  

Over 2 million total VOQUEZNA® prescriptions filled through October 16, 2026

 

  •  

Q3 2026 net revenues of $84.7 million increased 71% vs. Q3 2025 and 14% vs. Q2 2026

 

  •  

Generated positive operating cash flow for the second consecutive quarter

 

  •  

Reaffirmed full-year 2026 financial guidance

 

  •  

Conference call and webcast today, October 29, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. EDT

FLORHAM PARK, N.J., October 29, 2026 — Phathom Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: PHAT), a biopharmaceutical company focused on commercializing and developing novel treatments for gastrointestinal (GI) diseases, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

“We are pleased to report continued solid revenue growth and improved operating profitability for the third quarter,” said Steve Basta, President and Chief Executive Officer of Phathom. “We continue to grow VOQUEZNA prescribing depth among GI providers and have implemented several initiatives designed to make it easier for physicians to prescribe and patients to obtain our first-in-class GERD treatment. These efforts build on our commercial momentum, which includes recently reaching the milestone of two million VOQUEZNA prescriptions filled.”

“Our third quarter results reflect Phathom’s significant financial transformation, with revenue growth of 71% year over year and positive cash flow from operations for the second consecutive quarter,” said Sanjeev Narula, Chief Financial and Business Officer of Phathom. “We have established a stronger financial foundation through continued revenue growth and disciplined expense management, while maintaining the flexibility to invest in our commercial and clinical development priorities. We remain confident in our ability to meet our financial obligations and deliver on our full-year 2026 guidance.”

Recent Business Highlights and Third Quarter 2026 Results

VOQUEZNA Commercial Progress:

 

  •  

Over 2 million total VOQUEZNA prescriptions have been filled since launch through October 16, 2026. After achieving the first million prescriptions just over two years into launch, Phathom reached its second million filled prescriptions less than a year later.

 

  •  

Approximately 380,000 total VOQUEZNA prescriptions were filled during the third quarter 2026, including approximately 248,000 covered prescriptions and 132,000 cash-pay prescriptions.

 

  •  

Covered prescriptions in the third quarter amounted to approximately 65% of total quarterly prescriptions. Covered prescription volume increased approximately 19% compared to the second quarter 2026. Cash-pay prescription volume increased approximately 6% sequentially.

 

  •  

Phathom implemented several initiatives designed to improve VOQUEZNA access dynamics, including its “Access First” field messaging and promotional materials, and at the end of the third quarter, the introduction of a new copay offering through Phathom’s pharmacy partner, BlinkRx, under which eligible commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 for VOQUEZNA.

Clinical and Regulatory Updates:

 

  •  

VOQUEZNA Phase 3 As-Needed Program

 

  •  

Building on previously reported positive Phase 2 results, on September 29, 2026, Phathom announced the initiation of pHalcon-NERD-302, a registrational Phase 3 study of VOQUEZNA for as-needed (PRN) use in Non-Erosive Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (NERD). The first subject has been dosed, and sites are currently enrolling across the U.S. Topline results are expected in the first quarter 2028.

 

  •  

If successful, this Phase 3 program could support a potential label expansion for VOQUEZNA, which, if approved, could offer a differentiated treatment option and potentially expand the market opportunity for VOQUEZNA, by providing flexibility for patients with NERD to take treatment as-needed instead of chronic daily dosing.

 

  •  

Eosinophilic Esophagitis (EoE)

 

  •  

Phathom expects to report topline results during the fourth quarter 2026 for the 12-week blinded treatment portion of its Phase 2 pHalcon-EoE-201 trial evaluating VOQUEZNA in patients with Eosinophilic Esophagitis (EoE).

 

  •  

VOQUEZNA Data at ACG 2026

 

  •  

At the American College of Gastroenterology’s (ACG) 2026 Annual Scientific Meeting, Phathom presented new data highlighting the clinical and potential economic value of VOQUEZNA in GERD. A new predictive cost-effectiveness analysis projected VOQUEZNA to be cost-effective compared with proton pump inhibitors (PPIs) for the healing and maintenance of Erosive GERD in adults, with potential healthcare cost savings at the estimated net price based on clinical healing outcomes and fewer anticipated downstream interventions required.

 

  •  

Vonoprazan, the active drug in VOQUEZNA, was also the subject of 38 independent, investigator-initiated abstracts presented at ACG 2026, reflecting continued scientific interest across GERD, H. pylori eradication, and other gastrointestinal conditions.

 

  •  

VOQUEZNA Regulatory Exclusivity

 

  •  

On October 26, 2026, the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia granted Phathom’s motion to intervene in the litigation filed by BDR Pharmaceuticals against the FDA regarding VOQUEZNA’s regulatory exclusivity. On October 23, 2026, FDA opened a public comment docket on issues raised in the litigation, with comments due November 23, 2026.

 

  •  

Phathom believes the FDA carefully and correctly evaluated this issue in its June 6, 2025 decision granting Phathom’s December 11, 2024 Citizen Petition, in which FDA recognized 10 years of NCE exclusivity for VOQUEZNA tablets through May 3, 2032, as reflected in the Orange Book. Phathom intends to vigorously defend its statutory exclusivities.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results:

 

  •  

Revenue: Net revenues for the third quarter 2026 were $84.7 million, an increase of $35.2 million compared to $49.5 million for third quarter 2025. The increase was due to continued growth from execution of Phathom’s commercial strategy.

 

  •  

Research and development (R&D) expenses: R&D expenses for the third quarter 2026 were $8.7 million, an increase of $1.7 million compared to $7.0 million for third quarter 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher clinical development and regulatory costs.

 

  •  

Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses: SG&A expenses for the third quarter 2026 were $57.7 million, an increase of $6.1 million compared to $51.6 million for third quarter 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher promotional and personnel-related expenses.

  •  

Operating expenses: Operating expenses for the third quarter 2026 were $66.4 million, compared to $58.6 million for third quarter 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to higher clinical development costs associated with Phathom’s ongoing and planned clinical programs, as well as increased commercial investment supporting its sales force and Access First initiatives. Third quarter 2026 operating expenses included non-cash stock-based compensation of $5.9 million, compared to $9.3 million in the prior-year period. Non-GAAP operating expenses, excluding stock-based compensation, were $60.5 million, compared to $49.3 million for third quarter 2025.

 

  •  

Net loss: Net loss for the third quarter 2026 was $13.9 million, compared to $30.0 million for third quarter 2025. Non-GAAP adjusted net income for the third quarter 2026 was $3.0 million compared to a non-GAAP adjusted net loss of $11.2 million for the same period in 2025. These non-GAAP adjusted amounts, as more fully described below under “Non-GAAP Financial Measures,” exclude non-cash stock-based compensation charges, non-cash interest expense related to the accounting for our revenue interest financing liability, which is in excess of the actual interest owed, and interest expense related to the amortization of debt discount on our term loan. A reconciliation of the GAAP financial results to non-GAAP financial results is included in the tables below.

 

  •  

Cash and cash equivalents: As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents were $184.6 million, an increase of $2.1 million compared to the second quarter ended June 30, 2026. Phathom continues to believe cash on hand along with anticipated future cash generated from operations will be sufficient to invest in its business and satisfy all outstanding debt obligations without requiring additional debt or equity financing.

In particular, Phathom has provided non-GAAP operating profitability, operating expense, adjusted net loss and adjusted net loss per share, adjusted to exclude the items below. Non-GAAP financial measures are not an alternative to financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. However, Phathom believes the presentation of non-GAAP operating profitability, adjusted operating expense, net loss and adjusted net loss per share, when viewed in conjunction with GAAP results, provides investors with a more meaningful understanding of ongoing operating performance. Non-GAAP operating profitability and non-GAAP operating expense exclude non-cash stock-based compensation, which is impacted by changes in the market price of common stock. Adjusted net loss and net loss per share exclude (i) non-cash stock-based compensation, (ii) interest expense related to the accounting for our revenue interest financing liability, which is in excess of the actual interest

owed, and (iii) interest expense related to the amortization of debt discount on our term loan. Phathom does not provide a reconciliation of projected non-GAAP operating profitability or operating expense to GAAP operating expense due to the inherent difficulty in forecasting and quantifying non-cash stock-based compensation which is dependent on changes in the market price of common stock and necessary for such reconciliation.

Phathom believes the presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures provides useful information to management and investors regarding Phathom’s results of operations. When GAAP financial measures are viewed in conjunction with these non-GAAP financial measures, investors are provided with a more meaningful understanding of Phathom’s ongoing operating performance and are better able to compare Phathom’s performance between periods. In addition, these non-GAAP financial measures are among those indicators Phathom uses as a basis for evaluating performance, and planning and forecasting future periods. These non-GAAP financial measures are not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for GAAP financial measures. About Phathom Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

Phathom Pharmaceuticals is a biopharmaceutical company focused on the commercialization and development of novel treatments for gastrointestinal diseases. Phathom has in-licensed the exclusive rights to vonoprazan, a first-in-class potassium-competitive acid blocker (PCAB) for the U.S., Europe and Canada. Phathom currently markets vonoprazan in the United States as VOQUEZNA® (vonoprazan) tablets for the relief of heartburn associated with Non-Erosive GERD in adults, the healing and maintenance of healing of Erosive GERD in adults and relief of associated heartburn, and as part of VOQUEZNA® DUAL PAK® (vonoprazan tablets, amoxicillin capsules) and VOQUEZNA® TRIPLE PAK® (vonoprazan tablets, amoxicillin capsules, clarithromycin tablets) for the treatment of H. pylori infection in adults. For more information about Phathom, visit the company’s website at www.phathompharma.com and follow on LinkedIn and X.
