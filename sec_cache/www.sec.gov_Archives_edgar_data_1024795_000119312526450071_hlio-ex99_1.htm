Helios Technologies Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results; Consistent Execution Drives Record Third Quarter Sales and Increased Full Year 2026 Sales Outlook

•
Grew 3Q26 sales to a third quarter record $230 million, up 4% over prior year period or 13% growth on a pro-forma1 basis; Hydraulics +15% and Electronics +9% pro forma
•
Expanded 3Q26 gross profit over prior year period by $3 million, gross margin of 33.2% was 10 bps higher than prior year period
•
Delivered 3Q26 diluted EPS of $0.53 up 71% and adjusted diluted EPS of $0.80, up 11% over prior year period
•
Generated a third quarter record cash from operations of $40 million, up 59% over prior year period, and $32 million of free cash flow in 3Q26
•
Closed strategic acquisition in 3Q26 that expands Electronics capabilities and provides additional pathways for growth across multiple end markets
•
Repurchased $19 million of Company's common stock in the 3Q26; Paid 119th consecutive cash dividend
•
Following quarter end, achieved important milestone by securing Faster’s first thermal management order, demonstrating continued execution against Helios’ planned and disciplined data center market-entry strategy
•
Raised the FY2026 sales outlook; implies the Company's highest annual sales in its history

SARASOTA, FL, November 5, 2026 — Helios Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: HLIO) (“Helios” or the “Company”), a global leader in highly engineered motion control and electronic controls technology, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended October 3, 2026.

“We delivered a record third quarter, with sales above the high end of our outlook and adjusted earnings exceeding expectations. Both Hydraulics and Electronics contributed to the performance, demonstrating the breadth of our growth and the effectiveness of our customer-focused commercial strategy. Our order book provides solid visibility as we enter the fourth quarter, while our teams continue to execute through persistent inflationary pressures and a dynamic supply environment. This consistent performance reflects the progress we have made strengthening the business and our ability to deliver profitable growth as comparisons become more demanding,” said Sean Bagan, President & Chief Executive Officer of Helios.

“We also continued to translate The CORE Strategy into tangible commercial and operational progress. We completed Faster’s North American consolidation in Toledo, Ohio, and advanced additional footprint optimization initiatives designed to increase productivity, strengthen our regional capabilities, and reduce tariff exposure. In Electronics, we began a new multi-year relationship with Indian Motorcycle, supported by our strategic acquisition of select Radio Sound assets, which expanded our audio and communications capabilities and gives us a platform to grow with additional customers and markets. In Hydraulics, Faster secured its first thermal management order following quarter end, delivering on our stated objective ahead of year-end and validating our planned and methodical approach to entering this attractive market. These actions collectively demonstrate how we are combining organic investment, operational excellence, and disciplined capital deployment to strengthen Helios’ long-term growth platform,” he concluded.

Raising Full Year 2026 Sales Outlook, Tightening Bottom Line; Establishing Fourth Quarter 2026 Outlook2

Jeremy Evans, Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer, commented, “Our third quarter results reflect continued progress against our 2026 financial priorities. We grew sales 13% on a pro-forma basis to a third quarter record, with both segments growing over the year ago period. Third quarter sales and adjusted diluted EPS exceeded the high end of our guidance range partially due to timing of customer orders driven by announced price increases. We generated a third quarter record cash from operations of $40 million. Our cash continues to fund both growth investments, including our Electronics acquisition, and returns to shareholders through our 119th consecutive quarterly dividend and $19 million of share repurchases in the quarter. We continue to expect growth in the fourth quarter to moderate from the third quarter pace. Even so, our year-to-date performance and recent acquisition support raising our full year sales outlook implying the highest annual sales in Helios’ history. We are tightening our adjusted EBITDA margin and adjusted diluted EPS ranges and have updated them to layer in the acquisition and to reflect the inflationary cost environment. We expect the benefits of our recent pricing actions to show more fully in 2027, and we expect to realize the cost synergies from our Radio Sound acquisition starting in early 2027. We remain disciplined as we navigate a dynamic macro environment, finish 2026, and continue advancing toward The CORE Strategy’s 2030 targets."

About Helios Technologies

Helios Technologies is a global leader in highly engineered motion control and electronic controls technology, providing premium products that ensure safety, reliability, and seamless connectivity to diverse end markets including agriculture, construction, data centers, energy, health and wellness, industrial, marine, material handling, and recreational vehicles. Helios sells its products to customers in over 80 countries around the world. Its strategy is to be a diversified, customer-centric global enterprise distinguished by innovation, operational speed, and a high-performance culture. The Company has paid a cash dividend to its shareholders every quarter since becoming a public company in 1997.
