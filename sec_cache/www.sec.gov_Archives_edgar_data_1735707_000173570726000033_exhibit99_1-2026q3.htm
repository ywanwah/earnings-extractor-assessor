Garrett Motion Reports Strong Third Quarter 2026 Results and Announces $250 Million Share Repurchase Program for 2027
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Net sales totaled $930 million, up 3% on a constant currency* basis vs prior year, driven by strong performance in commercial vehicle and industrial
•Net income totaled $97 million; net income margin of 10.4%
•Adjusted EBIT* totaled $152 million; adjusted EBIT margin* of 16.3%
•Net cash provided by operating activities totaled $135 million
•Adjusted free cash flow* totaled $136 million
•Reaffirming 2026 full-year mid-point outlook

Third Quarter 2026 Business Highlights
•Continued growth in industrial above previous expectations
•Secured multiple turbo wins, including several light vehicle programs for both China domestic and export
•Secured several on- and off-highway commercial vehicle applications across all regions
•Secured a production award for industrial E-Cooling compressor in Asia
•E-Powertrain and E-Cooling compressor on track for start of production in 2027

PLYMOUTH, Mich. and ROLLE, Switzerland, October 29, 2026 – Garrett Motion Inc. (Nasdaq: GTX) ("Garrett" or the "Company"), a leading automotive and industrial technology provider, today announced its financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Additionally, the Company's Board of Directors approved a new share repurchase program for calendar year 2027, authorizing the Company to repurchase up to $250 million of common stock, and declared a cash dividend of $0.08 per share of common stock, payable on December 15, 2026, to shareholders of record as of December 1, 2026. The Company also executed a $50 million voluntary early debt repayment on its Term Loan in October.

“Our third quarter results reflect the strength of Garrett’s differentiated portfolio, disciplined execution and ability to grow ahead of the industry. We continued to secure new business in turbo, support growing demand for industrial applications and advance our zero emission technologies, while delivering strong profitability and cash flow. With this continued momentum we reaffirm our 2026 mid-point outlook, offsetting unfavorable foreign exchange, and remain focused on delivering long-term value for our shareholders,” said Olivier Rabiller, President and CEO of Garrett.

Results of Operations

Net sales for the third quarter of 2026 were $930 million, representing an increase of 3% (including an unfavorable impact of $3 million or 0.3% due to foreign currency translation) compared with $902 million in the third quarter of 2025. Gasoline decrease was primarily driven by demand softness in China, partially offset by program ramp-ups in Europe, Japan, Korea and India. Diesel increase was primarily driven by strong demand for light commercial vehicles and pickup trucks in Europe, North and South America and India. Commercial vehicle and industrial increases were primarily driven by strong demand in North America and China for industrial and off-highway applications combined with higher on-highway demand in Japan, Korea and India. Aftermarket increase was primarily driven by higher demand for replacement parts in North America off-highway and industrial and stronger demand for passenger vehicles replacement parts in Europe.

Cost of goods sold for the third quarter of 2026 increased to $724 million from $716 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by $39 million related to product mix, $18 million of commodity, transportation and energy inflation, $6 million of higher RD&E costs and $5 million of foreign currency impact. This increase was partially offset by $24 million of higher productivity net of labor inflation and repositioning costs, $19 million of lower import tariffs and $17 million from lower sales volumes.

Gross profit totaled $206 million for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $186 million in the third quarter of 2025, with a gross profit percentage for the third quarter of 2026 of 22.2% as compared to 20.6% in the third quarter of 2025. This increase in gross profit was driven by $21 million of favorable product mix, $20 million of higher productivity net of labor inflation and repositioning costs, $18 million of price net of inflation pass-through and $1 million of import tariffs. These increases were partially offset by $18 million of commodity, transportation and energy inflation, $8 million from lower sales volumes, $8 million of foreign currency impact and $6 million of higher RD&E costs.

Selling, general and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses for the third quarter of 2026 increased to $58 million from $57 million in the third quarter of 2025. This increase was driven by $3 million of higher personnel costs, partially offset by $2 million of lower professional services and patent costs.

Other expense in the third quarter of 2026 was consistent with the third quarter of 2025.

Interest expense in the third quarter of 2026 was $27 million as compared to $29 million in the third quarter of 2025. This decrease was primarily due to lower interest expense due to a different notional amount of debt outstanding during the period.

Non-operating expense (income) for the third quarter of 2026 was expense of $1 million as compared to income of $3 million in the third quarter of 2025, with the change in non-operating expense driven by the finalization of a tax indemnification obligation.

Tax expense for the third quarter of 2026 was $22 million as compared to $25 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily because of a decrease in U.S. taxes on international operations during 2026, the global mix of earnings from year-to-year, a one-time benefit related to the revaluation of deferred tax assets in China during 2025, and deductions related to employee share-based compensation during 2026.

Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $97 million as compared to $77 million in the third quarter of 2025 primarily driven by $20 million of increased gross profit, $3 million of lower tax expense, and $2 million of lower interest expense, partially offset by $4 million of higher non-operating expense (income) and $1 million of higher SG&A expense.

Net cash provided by operating activities totaled $135 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $100 million in the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of $35 million. The increase was primarily driven by $28 million of higher net income net of non-cash charges and $15 million of favorable impacts from changes in other assets and liabilities, partially offset by $8 million from unfavorable working capital changes.

Adjusted EBIT increased to $152 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $133 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase of $19 million was driven by $21 million of favorable product mix impact, $19 million pricing net of inflation pass-through and $18 million higher productivity, SG&A net. This increase was partially offset by $18 million commodity, transportation and energy inflation, $8 million of lower sales volume, $7 million of unfavorable foreign currency impact and $6 million higher RD&E costs.

Adjusted free cash flow was $136 million in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $107 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by $19 million higher Adjusted EBIT, $8 million lower cash taxes, $5 million lower cash interest and $3 million favorable impact from working capital (net of factoring), partially offset by $5 million higher capital expenditures.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

As of September 30, 2026, Garrett had $841 million in available liquidity, including $211 million in unrestricted cash and cash equivalents and $630 million of undrawn commitments under its revolving credit facility. As of December 31, 2025, Garrett had $807 million in available liquidity, including $177 million in unrestricted cash and cash equivalents and $630 million of undrawn commitments under its revolving credit facility.

As of September 30, 2026, total principal amount of debt outstanding was $1,384 million, compared to $1,439 million as of December 31, 2025.

During the third quarter of 2026, we repurchased $57 million of our common stock under our authorized share repurchase program and we had remaining repurchase capacity of $78 million as of September 30, 2026.

About Garrett Motion Inc.
A differentiated technology leader, Garrett Motion has a 70-year history of innovation in the automotive sector (cars, trucks) and beyond (off-highway equipment, marine, power generators). Its well-recognized expertise in turbocharging has enabled significant reductions in engine size, fuel consumption, and CO2 emissions. Garrett is committed to advancing turbo applications while leveraging its unique technology solutions, such as fuel cell compressors for hydrogen fuel cell vehicles, as well as electric propulsion and thermal management systems for automotive and industrial applications. (1)We evaluate performance on the basis of Adjusted EBIT and Adjusted EBITDA. We define “EBIT” as our net income calculated in accordance with U.S. GAAP, plus the sum of (i) interest expense net of interest income and (ii) tax expense. We define Adjusted EBIT as EBIT, plus the sum of (i) repositioning costs, (ii) foreign exchange (gain) loss on debt net of related hedging gain/loss, (iii) discounting costs on factoring, (iv) gain on sale of equity investment, (v) acquisition and divestiture expenses, (vi) other non-operating income, and (vii) debt refinancing and redemption costs, if any. We define Adjusted EBITDA as EBIT, plus the sum of (i) repositioning costs, (ii) foreign exchange (gain) loss on debt net of related hedging gain/loss, (iii) discounting costs on factoring, (iv) gain on sale of equity investment, (v) acquisition and divestiture expenses, (vi) other non-operating income, and (vii) debt refinancing and redemption costs, if any, plus (viii) depreciation and (ix) stock compensation expense. We believe that Adjusted EBIT and Adjusted EBITDA are important indicators of operating performance and provide useful information for investors because:
•Adjusted EBIT and Adjusted EBITDA exclude the effects of income taxes, as well as the effects of financing activities by eliminating the effects of interest;
•certain adjustment items, while periodically affecting our results, may vary significantly from period to period and have a disproportionate effect in a given period, which affects the comparability of our results; and
•Adjusted EBITDA also excludes the effects of investing activities by eliminating the effects of depreciation.
In addition, our management may use Adjusted EBIT and Adjusted EBITDA in setting performance incentive targets to align performance measurement with operational performance.
