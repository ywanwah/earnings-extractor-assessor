Caleres Announces Famous Footwear Leadership Transition

Provides Update on Preliminary Third Quarter Results & Earnings Date

ST. LOUIS, Nov. 19, 2026 — Caleres (CAL: NYSE) today announced Brian Costello will retire as division president of Famous Footwear in the first quarter of 2027 as part of a planned leadership transition. David Law, currently general manager of Allen Edmonds, will succeed Costello as division president of Famous Footwear. Costello and Law will work closely together over the coming months to support an orderly transition.

“I want to thank Brian for his leadership and progress strengthening the brand and merchandise assortment and the team at Famous Footwear,” said Jay Schmidt, president and chief executive officer of Caleres. “David is a results-driven leader who brings an extensive retail background and a strong track record of success at Caleres to the role at Famous Footwear.”

Law joined Caleres in 2021 as senior vice president and general manager of Allen Edmonds, and in 2026 he also took on leadership of the company’s Specialty Stores Center of Expertise. Law brings over 25 years of footwear, retail and merchandising experience to the role. Prior to joining Caleres, he spent a decade at Lord & Taylor, where he worked his way up from buying to chief merchant. He also held leadership roles at Hudson’s Bay, PVH and Vida Shoes.

In conjunction with this announcement, Caleres provides an update on third quarter earnings expectations. Caleres expects to report third quarter sales of $792.2 million and adjusted earnings per diluted share two cents below the low end of its previously issued guidance range of $0.62 to $0.70. Famous Footwear sales declined 3.3% with comparable store sales down 2.6%, and Brand Portfolio sales increased 3.0%. Based on current trends, the company still expects to achieve its most recent annual adjusted earnings per diluted share guidance of $1.50 to $1.65.

About Caleres

Caleres is a market-leading portfolio of global footwear brands that include Famous Footwear, Sam Edelman, Stuart Weitzman, Allen Edmonds, Naturalizer and Vionic. Our products are available virtually everywhere — in the ~1,000 retail stores we operate, in hundreds of major department and specialty stores, on our branded e-Commerce sites, and on many additional third-party retail websites. Combined, these brands make Caleres a company with both a legacy and a mission. Our legacy is nearly 150 years of craftsmanship and our passion for fit, while our mission is to continue to inspire people to feel great… feet first. Visit caleres.com to learn more about us.
