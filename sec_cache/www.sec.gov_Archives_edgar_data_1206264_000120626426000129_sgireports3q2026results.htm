SOMNIGROUP INTERNATIONAL INC. REPORTS RECORD THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
- Third Quarter Net Sales increased 11% to $2.4 Billion
- Third Quarter GAAP EPS of $0.74, decreased 10.8%, and Adjusted EPS(1) of $1.04, increased 9.5%
- Strong Third Quarter Cash Flows from Operations of $349 Million

DALLAS, TX, November 9, 2026 - Somnigroup International Inc. (NYSE: SGI, "Company") announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and revised financial guidance for the full year 2026.

On August 26, 2026, the Company completed its previously announced acquisition of Leggett & Platt, Incorporated ("Leggett & Platt"). The Company's 2026 third quarter financials include Leggett & Platt's operations for the period from August 26, 2026 through September 30, 2026 (the "stub period").

THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL SUMMARY

•Total net sales increased 10.8% to a record $2,350.8 million as compared to $2,122.6 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by the inclusion of $372.6 million of Leggett & Platt net sales for the stub period.
•Gross margin was 42.0% as compared to 44.9% in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross margin(1) was 43.0% as compared to 45.6% in the third quarter of 2025.
•Operating income decreased 5.5% to $297.4 million as compared to $314.7 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by costs incurred during the quarter related to the Leggett & Platt acquisition. Adjusted operating income(1) increased 6.0% to a record $364.2 million as compared to $343.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•Net income decreased 7.8% to $163.5 million as compared to $177.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income(1) increased 13.7% to a record $228.9 million as compared to $201.4 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•Earnings per diluted share ("EPS") decreased 10.8% to $0.74 as compared to $0.83 in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EPS(1) increased 9.5% to a record $1.04 as compared to $0.95 in the third quarter of 2025.
•Leverage based on the ratio of consolidated indebtedness less netted cash(1) to adjusted EBITDA(1) was 2.69 times for the trailing twelve months ended September 30, 2026 compared to 3.28 times for the trailing twelve months ended September 30, 2025.

Business Segment Highlights

The Company's business segments include Mattress Firm, Leggett & Platt (acquired on August 26, 2026), Tempur Sealy North America and Tempur Sealy International. Corporate operating expenses are not included in any of the business segments and are presented separately as a reconciling item to consolidated results.

Mattress Firm net sales decreased 5.8% to $1,008.3 million as compared to $1,070.8 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by softer online sales. Mattress Firm same store sales were down slightly as compared to the third quarter of 2025. All Mattress Firm sales are reported through the direct bedding channel.

Mattress Firm gross margin was 34.4%, consistent with the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross margin(1) declined 100 basis points to 34.6% as compared to 35.6% in the third quarter of 2025. Gross margin results were primarily driven by investments in Mattress Firm's stores and consumer financing costs, partially offset by the achievement of cost synergies.

Mattress Firm operating margin was 9.4% as compared to 7.6% in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted operating margin(1) improved 20 basis points to 9.6% as compared to 9.4% in the third quarter of 2025. These improvements were primarily driven by favorable co-operative advertising expense, partially offset by the decline in adjusted gross margin.

Leggett & Platt net sales were $372.6 million for the stub period, with $271.3 million reported in the wholesale bedding and furnishings channel and $101.3 million reported in the specialized products channel.

Leggett & Platt gross margin was 17.7% for the stub period. Adjusted gross margin(1) was 21.9% for the stub period.

Leggett & Platt operating margin was 5.6% for the stub period. Adjusted operating margin(1) was 11.0% for the stub period.

Tempur Sealy North America net sales decreased 5.8% to $693.1 million as compared to $736.1 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by market conditions. Net sales through the wholesale bedding and furnishings channel decreased $44.9 million, or 7.2%, to $581.5 million as compared to the third quarter of 2025. Reported net sales through the direct bedding channel increased $1.9 million, or 1.7%, to $111.6 million as compared to the third quarter of 2025.

North America gross margin was 64.7% as compared to 58.4% in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross margin(1) improved 690 basis points to 65.5% as compared to 58.6% in the third quarter of 2025. These improvements were primarily driven by tariff refunds and the achievement of synergies from the Mattress Firm acquisition. These improvements were partially offset by inflation.

North America operating margin was 33.2% as compared to 29.1% in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted operating margin(1) improved 450 basis points to 34.0% as compared to 29.5% in the third quarter of 2025. These improvements were primarily driven by the improvement in gross margin, partially offset by investments in co-operative advertising expense.

Tempur Sealy International net sales decreased 12.3% to $276.8 million as compared to $315.7 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by decreased net sales at Dreams of 34.7% following the implementation of a new ERP system, leading to operational disruption throughout the third quarter, amid challenging market conditions. Tempur Sealy International (excluding Dreams) grew high single digits as compared to the third quarter of 2025. On a constant currency basis(1), International net sales decreased 11.7% as compared to the third quarter of 2025. Net sales through the direct bedding channel decreased $42.9 million, or 21.2%, to $159.9 million as compared to the third quarter of 2025. Net sales through the wholesale bedding and furnishings channel increased $4.0 million, or 3.5%, to $116.9 million as compared to the third quarter of 2025.

International gross margin declined 360 basis points to 45.5% as compared to 49.1% in the third quarter of 2025. The decline was primarily driven by temporary deleverage at Dreams and mix.    

International operating margin declined 870 basis points to 9.4% as compared to 18.1% in the third quarter of 2025. The decline was primarily driven by operating expense deleverage and the decline in gross margin.

Corporate operating expense increased to $74.2 million as compared to $37.9 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by costs related to the Leggett & Platt acquisition. Adjusted operating expense(1) was $34.6 million as compared to $31.1 million in the third quarter of 2025.

Consolidated Financial Position

Consolidated net income decreased 7.8% to $163.5 million as compared to $177.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income(1) increased 13.7% to a record $228.9 million as compared to $201.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. EPS decreased 10.8% to $0.74 as compared to $0.83 in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EPS(1) increased 9.5% to a record $1.04 as compared to $0.95 in the third quarter of 2025.

The Company ended the third quarter of 2026 with total debt of $5.6 billion and consolidated indebtedness less netted cash(1) of $5.0 billion. Leverage based on the ratio of consolidated indebtedness less netted cash(1) to adjusted EBITDA(1) was 2.69 times for the trailing twelve months ended September 30, 2026.

About Somnigroup International Inc.

Somnigroup (NYSE: SGI) is the world's leading bedding company, dedicated to transforming how the world sleeps. With superior capabilities in design, component and finished goods manufacturing, distribution and retail, we deliver breakthrough sleep solutions and serve the evolving needs of consumers in more than 100 countries worldwide through our fully-owned businesses, Tempur Sealy, Mattress Firm, Leggett & Platt and Dreams. Our portfolio includes the most highly recognized brands in the industry, including Tempur-Pedic®, Sealy®, Stearns & Foster® and Sleepy's®, enhanced by Leggett & Platt's diversified component engineering expertise. Our global omni-channel platform enables us to meet consumers wherever they shop, offering a personal connection and innovation to provide a unique retail experience and tailored solutions.

These references to constant currency basis do not include operational impacts that could result from fluctuations in foreign currency rates.
