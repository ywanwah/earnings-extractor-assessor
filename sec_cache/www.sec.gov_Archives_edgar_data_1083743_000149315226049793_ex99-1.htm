Flux Power Reports 2027 Fiscal First Quarter Financial Results

 

Vista, CA — November 3, 2026 — Flux Power Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: FLUX) (“Flux Power” or the “Company”), a leading developer of advanced lithium-ion energy storage solutions and fleet intelligence technology, today reported financial and operational results for the 2027 fiscal first quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

2027 Fiscal First Quarter and Recent Business Highlights

 

  ● Revenue for the 2027 fiscal first quarter was $6.8 million
  ● Operating expenses for the 2027 fiscal first quarter decreased by 22% from the 2026 fiscal first quarter
  ● Received an order from our largest material handling customer
  ● Continued to expand reach in the new market vertical of Robotics with a global technology platform customer
  ● Accelerated dealer engagement and momentum following achievement of Hyster-Yale certification last quarter
  ● Announced three product innovations spanning battery safety with integrated gas detection, expanded material handing applications to additional truck models and equipment categories, and extended connected fleet intelligence with battery location and intelligent charging alerts
  ● Guiding 2027 fiscal second quarter revenue to be in the range of $7 million to $8 million

 

CEO Commentary

 

“We continued to make meaningful progress across our key operational and sales initiatives, executing with discipline and positioning the Company for accelerated growth,” said Krishna Vanka, CEO of Flux Power. “I am proud that the team delivered on our $6.8 million revenue commitment this quarter, despite facing multiple headwinds across the business. I am also pleased to announce that our most significant customer who previously paused orders returned with a meaningful order through our OEM partner. This win along with a number of orders through our OEM partners for first-time Flux Power customers demonstrate the initial success of our OEM partnership strategy.”

 

To complement these efforts, we launched a go-to-market campaign targeting the 500 largest end-user accounts in North America. This campaign adds a direct, enterprise-level dialogue with the operators of the largest fleets. We are also converting early wins and securing follow-on orders in high-growth new verticals, such as Robotics, with our initial global technology platform customer. We are also leveraging our S-Series as an anchor product for white-label opportunities and advancing a more integrated supply-chain model that is designed to improve scale and gross margin potential.

 

“Taken together, these developments reflect a commercial strategy that we believe is beginning to compound. With a leaner cost structure and growing market demand for lithium-ion battery solutions, I believe we’re in a much stronger position to drive growth and move toward profitability in the coming quarters.”

 

 

 

 

2027 Fiscal First Quarter Financial Results

 

Revenue for the 2027 fiscal first fiscal quarter was $6.8 million, compared to $8.2 million in the prior quarter and $13.2 million in the same quarter a year ago. Gross profit for the fiscal first quarter of 2027 was $1.7 million, or 24.8% of revenue, compared to $2.3 million, or 27.4% of revenue, in the prior quarter and $3.8 million, or 28.6% of revenue, in same quarter a year ago.

 

Operating expenses for the 2027 fiscal first quarter were $4.6 million, compared to $4.4 million in the prior quarter and $5.9 million in the same quarter a year ago. The year-over-year decrease in operating expenses primarily reflects the benefit of previous actions to reduce headcount and streamline the operating model.

 

Operating loss for the 2027 fiscal first quarter was $3.0 million, compared to an operating loss of $2.1 million in the prior quarter and an operating loss of $2.2 million in the same quarter a year ago. Excluding costs associated with stock-based compensation, the 2027 fiscal first quarter non-GAAP operating loss was $2.8 million, compared to a non-GAAP operating loss of $1.9 million in the prior quarter and $2.0 million in the same quarter a year ago.

 

Net loss for the 2027 fiscal first quarter was $3.2 million, or ($0.15) per share, compared to a net loss of $2.3 million, or ($0.11) per share, in the prior quarter and a net loss of $2.6 million, or ($0.15) per share, in the same quarter a year ago. Excluding the above-referenced stock-based compensation costs, the 2027 fiscal first quarter non-GAAP net loss was $3.0 million, or ($0.14) per share, compared to a non-GAAP net loss of $2.1 million, or ($0.10) per share, in the prior quarter and a non-GAAP net loss of $2.4 million, or ($0.14) per share, in the same quarter a year ago.

 

Adjusted EBITDA for the 2027 fiscal first quarter was negative $2.6 million, compared to a negative $1.6 million in the prior quarter and a negative $1.7 million in the same quarter a year ago.

 

Balance Sheet

 

Cash as of September 30, 2026 was $0.4 million, compared to $0.3 million as of June 30, 2026. Flux Power is currently evaluating potential financing alternatives and strategic partnerships to ensure the Company has the financial flexibility necessary to continue executing on its operating strategy.

About Flux Power

 

Flux Power (NASDAQ: FLUX) designs, manufactures, and sells advanced lithium-ion energy storage solutions for electrification of a range of industrial and commercial sectors including material handling and airport ground support equipment (GSE). Flux Power’s lithium-ion battery packs, including the proprietary battery management system (BMS) and telemetry, provide customers with a better performing, lower cost of ownership, and more environmentally friendly alternative, in many instances, to traditional lead acid and propane-based solutions. Lithium-ion battery packs reduce CO2 emissions and help improve sustainability and ESG metrics for fleets. For more information, please visit www.fluxpower.com.
