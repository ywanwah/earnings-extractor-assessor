BANK OF MARIN BANCORP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS DRIVEN BY LOAN GROWTH, NIM EXPANSION AND HIGHER EARNINGS


NOVATO, CA, October 26, 2026 - Bank of Marin Bancorp, "Bancorp" (Nasdaq: BMRC), parent company of Bank of Marin, "Bank," completed another quarter of improved financial performance, reflecting continued enhanced profitability and overall balance sheet strength. This performance was supported by progress across key financial and operating priorities:

•Increased earnings per share, pre-tax pre-provision net income, and return on average tangible common equity
•Expanded net interest margin from higher loan yields and reduced deposit costs, and grew both total deposits and noninterest-bearing deposits
•Loan growth from organic originations supplemented by a loan pool purchase
•Core non-interest income growth
•Prudent expense management

BMRC reports net income of $9.9 million for the third quarter of 2026. This compares to net income of $9.2 million for the second quarter of 2026 and net income of $7.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. Diluted earnings per share was $0.62 for the third quarter, compared to $0.58 for the prior quarter and $0.47 for the third quarter of the prior year. Continued net interest margin expansion largely drove these increases, contributing to a 32% year-over-year increase in quarterly diluted earnings per share.

"Active management of deposit costs, growth in fee income, and expense discipline drove a 17% increase in pre-tax pre-provision net income on a sequential quarter basis," said President & CEO Tim Myers. "Organic originations drove loan growth during the quarter which was supplemented by a California residential loan pool purchase that utilized excess liquidity, diversified our earning assets, and is expected to provide attractive risk-adjusted returns."

Continued legacy loan repricing along with new loan originations at higher rates should support modest net interest margin expansion over time."

Cash, Cash Equivalents and Restricted Cash

Total cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash were $265.3 million at September 30, 2026, a decrease of $14.3 million compared to $279.6 million at June 30, 2026.

Investments

The investment securities portfolio totaled $1.215 billion at September 30, 2026, a decrease of $27.6 million from June 30, 2026. The decrease in the portfolio was due to principal repayments and calls/maturities totaling $50.2 million and $1.1 million, respectively, and an increase of $35.2 million in unrealized losses on available-for-sale ("AFS") securities, partially offset by purchases of $58.9 million. The portfolio is eligible for pledging to the Federal Home Loan Bank ("FHLB") and the Federal Reserve as collateral for borrowing, and is comprised of high credit quality investments with an average effective duration of 3.11. Deposits

Deposits increased $25.3 million, or 0.8%, to $3.395 billion at September 30, 2026, compared to $3.370 billion at June 30, 2026, including a $45.0 million increase in noninterest-bearing balances, partially offset by decreases in money market and time accounts. As of September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026, there were no one-way sales, although the Bank sold an average of $130.6 million in the third quarter, compared to an average of $93.7 million in the second quarter, within money market accounts during the quarter which enhanced non-interest income and net interest margin. Noninterest-bearing deposits continued to make up a strong 37.8% of total deposits at September 30, 2026, compared to 36.7% at June 30, 2026. The Bank's focused business development efforts generated $23.2 million in new deposit relationships during the quarter, including $13.3 million in new noninterest-bearing deposits.

Earnings

Net Interest Income

Net interest income totaled $31.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, a $619 thousand increase from the prior quarter. This was driven by an increase of $834 thousand in interest income on loans, largely due to an 11 basis point increase in average loan yields due to growth at higher rates. Also contributing to the increase was the reduction of $527 thousand in interest expense on deposits, due to improved deposit mix and active balance sheet management through one-way sales of deposits. Average balances on interest-bearing transaction accounts decreased in the quarter while average balances of noninterest-bearing balances increased. The increases were partially offset by a $505 thousand decrease in interest income from investment securities due to paydowns.

The net interest margin increased 10 basis points to 3.48% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 3.38% for the prior quarter. The increase is primarily due to the 11 basis point increase in average loan yields, a four basis point decrease in cost of deposits, and the use of one-way sales of deposits, which improved the mix of average assets and liabilities. Newly funded loans were originated at a weighted average rate of 12 basis points higher than prior quarter.

Non-Interest Income

Non-interest income was $3.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $3.2 million for the prior quarter. The increase of $501 thousand was primarily attributable to an increase of $251 thousand in service charges on business deposit accounts due to an updated pricing strategy effective July 1st, and an increase in dividend income due to a special dividend on FHLB stock of $199 thousand.

Non-Interest Expense

Non-interest expense totaled $20.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $21.6 million for the prior quarter, a decrease of $1.0 million, primarily driven by a decrease of $465 thousand in salaries and related benefits expense which included an adjustment to the incentive bonus accrual as well as the impact of a decreased number of full-time equivalent employees at the end of the third quarter. Also contributing to the decrease in quarterly expense was a $239 thousand reduction in Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation insurance expense during the quarter, a decrease of $263 thousand in other expense, and a decrease of $132 thousand in professional services mostly related to audit, operations, compliance, information security and accounting fees.

Share Repurchase Program

On July 24, 2025, the Board of Directors authorized the repurchase of up to $25.0 million of its common stock effective July 24, 2025 through July 31, 2027. There were no repurchases in the third quarter of 2026 or in the second quarter of 2026. As of September 30, 2026, the amount remaining available for repurchase of shares was $23.9 million.

About Bank of Marin Bancorp

Founded in 1990 and headquartered in Novato, California, Bank of Marin is a wholly owned subsidiary of Bank of Marin Bancorp (Nasdaq: BMRC). With assets of $3.867 billion, Bank of Marin is a leading business and community bank providing commercial and personal banking, specialty lending, and wealth management and trust services through a network of 27 branches and eight commercial banking offices serving Northern California.

Known for delivering exceptional client service and investing in the communities it serves, Bank of Marin has been recognized as one of the San Francisco Business Times' Top Corporate Philanthropists since 2003 and ranked seventh on Sacramento Business Journal's 2026 Corporate Direct Giving List. Additional honors include being named one of North Bay Business Journal's Best Places to Work in 2026, the Bank's 17th recognition, and inclusion in Business Insider's America's High-Growth Companies 2026. Bank of Marin Bancorp, which was ranked the nation's No. 2 community bank deposit franchise by S&P Global Market Intelligence, is included in the Russell 2000® Small-Cap Index and the Nasdaq ABA Community Bank Index. For more information, visit www.bankofmarin.com.
