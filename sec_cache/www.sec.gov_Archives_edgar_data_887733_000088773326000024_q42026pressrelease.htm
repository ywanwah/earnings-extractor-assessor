CENTRAL GARDEN & PET REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FISCAL YEAR 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS AND PROVIDES FISCAL 2027 OUTLOOK

Reports fiscal 2026 net sales of $2.9 billion compared with $3.1 billion in the prior year
Grows fiscal 2026 organic net sales 1%
Delivers record fiscal 2026 GAAP EPS of $3.03 and record non-GAAP EPS of $3.00
Expects fiscal 2027 non-GAAP EPS of $2.65 or better

WALNUT CREEK, Calif. – Central Garden & Pet Company (NASDAQ: CENT) (NASDAQ: CENTA) ("Central"), a leading consumer goods company in the pet and garden industries, today announced results for its fourth quarter and fiscal year ended September 26, 2026.
“For the full year, we grew organic sales and gross margin and delivered record earnings per share in a more cautious consumer environment, reflecting sharp execution across our portfolio. Our teams delivered organic sales and profit growth in both Pet and Garden. We also announced the addition of two iconic businesses to our family of brands, Burpee in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, and TRIXIE, which is expected to close in the second quarter of fiscal 2027,” said Niko Lahanas, CEO of Central Garden & Pet. “We’re confident our Central to Home strategy and exceptional team position us to navigate near-term headwinds while continuing to invest in our long-term strategy.”

Fiscal 2026 Results
(All comparisons versus fiscal 2025)
Net sales decreased 6% to $2.9 billion, due to the exit of the pet distribution business at the beginning of the third quarter, while organic net sales increased 1%. Pet segment sales decreased 11% to $1.6 billion, while Pet organic sales increased 1%, led by growth in Professional and Dog Treats. Garden segment sales increased 1% to $1.3 billion, with growth in Fertilizer, Wild Bird, and Grass Seed.
Gross profit increased 1% to $1.0 billion, with gross margin expanding 240 basis points to 34.3%. On a non-GAAP basis, which excludes tariff refunds received during the year, gross profit decreased 1% to $990 million, with gross margin expanding 150 basis points to 33.6%, driven by mix improvement and cost savings.
SG&A was $724 million, a reduction of 3%. On a non-GAAP basis, SG&A was $705 million, a reduction of 5%, due to the exit of Central's pet distribution business and productivity improvements.
Operating income increased 14% to $286 million, with operating margin expanding 170 basis points to 9.7%. On a non-GAAP basis, operating income increased 8% to $285 million, with operating margin expanding 120 basis points to 9.7%, driven by improved gross margin and cost discipline.
Net interest expense was $29 million, compared with $33 million, benefiting from higher interest income.
Other expense was $7 million compared with $0.5 million, driven primarily by Central's share of initial losses from the pet distribution partnership.
Net income increased 15% to $188 million, or $3.03 per diluted share, an increase of $0.48. On a non-GAAP basis, net income increased 7% to $186 million, or $3.00 per diluted share, an increase of $0.27.
Adjusted EBITDA increased $16 million to $387 million, with margin expanding 130 basis points to 13.2%.
The effective tax rate was 24.5% compared with 24.4%.

Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results
(All comparisons versus Q4 FY 2025)
Net sales decreased 21% to $537 million, while organic net sales decreased 6%.
Gross profit increased 3% to $202 million, with gross margin expanding 870 basis points to 37.7%. On a non-GAAP basis, which excludes tariff refunds received during the fourth quarter, gross profit decreased 8% to $182 million, with gross margin expanding 480 basis points to 33.9%.

Operating income was $29 million, compared with an operating loss of $6 million, with operating margin expanding 630 basis points to 5.4%. On a non-GAAP basis, operating income was $11 million, compared with an operating loss of $1 million, with operating margin expanding 210 basis points to 2.0%.
Other expense increased $8 million to $9 million, driven primarily by Central's share of initial losses from the pet distribution partnership.
Net interest expense was $5 million, a reduction of $2 million.
Net income was $12 million, an increase of $21 million, or $0.19 per diluted share, an improvement of $0.35. On a non-GAAP basis, net loss was $2 million, an improvement of $3 million, or a loss of $0.04 per diluted share, an improvement of $0.05.
Adjusted EBITDA increased $10 million to $36 million, with margin expanding 290 basis points to 6.7%.

Pet Segment Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results
(All comparisons versus Q4 FY 2025)
Net sales in the Pet segment decreased 29% to $305 million, while Pet organic net sales decreased 5%. The decrease in organic net sales was due to softer consumer demand in certain categories, lost distribution in lower-margin pet beds and a temporary supply constraint in one of Central's pet businesses.
Operating income increased 85% to $51 million, with margin expanding 1,030 basis points to 16.7%. On a non-GAAP basis, operating income increased 7% to $33 million, with margin expanding 350 basis points to 10.7%.
Adjusted EBITDA increased $1 million to $42 million, with margin expanding 430 basis points to 13.8%.

Garden Segment Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results
(All comparisons versus Q4 FY 2025)
Net sales in the Garden segment decreased 7% to $232 million, due to unfavorable weather for Live Plants, and lower sales in Central's distribution business resulting from a vendor's decision to sell directly to retailers.
Operating income was $6 million, compared with an operating loss of $2 million, with margin expanding 330 basis points to 2.6%. On a non-GAAP basis, operating income was $6 million, an increase of $5 million, with margin expanding 220 basis points to 2.6%, primarily from improved mix and productivity savings.
Adjusted EBITDA increased $5 million to $16 million, with a margin expanding 240 basis points to 6.9%.

Liquidity and Debt
(All comparisons versus fiscal 2025)
Cash provided by operations was $316 million, compared with $333 million.
Cash and cash equivalents at September 26, 2026, were $1,092 million, an increase of $210 million.
Total debt was $1.2 billion, consistent with the prior year period, with strong liquidity supporting continued investment capacity and financial flexibility.
Central had no borrowings outstanding under its credit facility at year-end.
Gross leverage, calculated using the definitions for Indebtedness and EBITDA in Central's credit agreement, ended the year at 2.6x, below Central’s target range of 3.0 to 3.5x. Net leverage was 0.2x.

Non-GAAP Adjustments
In fiscal 2026, non-GAAP adjustments had a minimal net impact on operating income, as tariff refunds fully offset facility closure and business exit charges.
Central recognized incremental operating expense of $24 million, of which $14 million was recognized in its Pet segment, primarily related to the exit of a minor business and the closure of two facilities, and $10 million in its Garden segment related to remaining cost associated with the closure of five distribution centers in fiscal 2025 and 2024. Of the total charges $2 million was recognized in its Garden segment during the fourth quarter.
Offsetting these charges, Central recognized incremental gross profit of $24 million for tariff refunds received, of which $22 million was recognized in its Pet segment and $2 million in its Garden segment. Of these refunds, $20 million was received in the fourth quarter, comprising $18 million in its Pet segment and $2 million in its Garden segment.
For the full year, non-GAAP adjustments reduced net income by $2 million, primarily due to a third-quarter gain recorded in other expense, net from the contribution of its pet distribution business to the formation of a new partnership and additional interest income from tariff refunds received in the fourth quarter.

Acquisitions
•TRIXIE
In the fourth quarter, Central entered into a definitive agreement to acquire an 80% interest in TRIXIE Heimtierbedarf GmbH & Co. KG (“TRIXIE”), the leading European pet supplies and pet snacks company, for €340 million in cash at closing plus up to €60 million in additional earn-out payments, for total consideration of up to €400 million. Together, Central and TRIXIE will create the leading global player in pet supplies.
TRIXIE serves thousands of pet retail stores internationally with a broad portfolio of pet products, approximately 90% of which are own-brand. The transaction will give Central a differentiated presence with approximately 10% of net sales generated outside of the United States and establish a scaled platform to consolidate the fragmented European pet specialty market. Members of TRIXIE's leadership team will retain a minority stake and continue in key roles.

The transaction is expected to close in the second quarter of fiscal 2027.
•Burpee
After fiscal year end, Central acquired W. Atlee Burpee Company (“Burpee”), an iconic brand in the packet seed and young plant categories trusted by gardeners for 150 years. Burpee offers a comprehensive horticulture portfolio of seeds, plants and garden supplies.
Founded in 1876, Burpee is recognized as the premier brand in the category based on consumer awareness, satisfaction and loyalty. Burpee's comprehensive portfolio, positioned around helping home gardeners succeed, is sold across a diversified set of channels including mass merchandise, grocery and club, eCommerce and catalog, home improvement, and farm and fleet retailers. Burpee will operate as a separate entity and continue to be headquartered in Pennsylvania.
The transaction closed on September 30, 2026.

Outlook for Fiscal 2027
Central currently expects fiscal 2027 non-GAAP EPS of $2.65 or better. The outlook reflects a prudent view of current market conditions and continued investments to support long-term growth. It also anticipates a challenging first quarter, with opportunities for improvement as the year progresses.
The outlook includes Burpee, acquired on September 30, 2026, and TRIXIE, which is expected to close in the second quarter of fiscal 2027. Purchase accounting is expected to have a meaningful impact on both acquisitions in fiscal 2027, resulting in a minimal contribution to EPS. Central expects a more meaningful EPS contribution in fiscal 2028 as these impacts moderate.
Capital spending is projected at approximately $50 million, focused on maintenance, productivity, and targeted growth initiatives across both segments.
This outlook excludes the impact of any other acquisitions, divestitures or restructuring activities that may occur during fiscal 2027 and any further tariffs.

About Central Garden & Pet
Central Garden & Pet Company (NASDAQ: CENT) (NASDAQ: CENTA) is a leading consumer goods company in the pet and garden industries. Guided by the belief that home is central to life, the company's purpose is to proudly nurture happy and healthy homes. For over 45 years, its innovative and trusted solutions have helped lawns grow greener, gardens bloom bigger, pets live healthier, and communities grow stronger. Central is home to a diversified portfolio of market-leading brands including Amdro®, Aqueon®, Best Bully Sticks®, Cadet®, C&S®, Farnam®, Ferry-Morse®, Kaytee®, Nylabone®, Pennington®, Sevin® and Zoëcon®. With fiscal 2026 net sales of $2.9 billion, the company has strong manufacturing and logistics capabilities supported by a passionate, entrepreneurial growth culture that incorporates sustainability. Central is headquartered in Walnut Creek, California, and employs more than 5,200 people, primarily across North America. Visit www.central.com to learn more.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
We report our financial results in accordance with GAAP. However, to supplement the financial results prepared in accordance with GAAP, we use non-GAAP financial measures including non-GAAP net income and diluted net income per share, non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP gross profit and gross margin, non-GAAP selling, general and administrative expense and adjusted EBITDA. Management uses these non-GAAP financial measures that exclude the impact of specific items (described below) in making financial, operating and planning decisions and in evaluating our performance. Management believes that these non-GAAP financial measures may be useful to investors in their assessment of our ongoing operating performance and provide additional meaningful comparisons between current results and results in prior operating periods. While management believes that non-GAAP measures are useful supplemental information, such adjusted results are not intended to replace our GAAP financial results and should be read in conjunction with those GAAP results.
We have also provided organic net sales, a non-GAAP measure that excludes the impact of businesses purchased or exited in the prior 12 months, because we believe it permits investors to better understand the performance of our historical business without the impact of recent acquisitions or dispositions.
Adjusted EBITDA is defined by us as income before income tax, net other expense, net interest expense, depreciation and amortization and stock-based compensation expense (or operating income plus depreciation and amortization expense and stock-based compensation expense). Adjusted EBITDA further excludes one-time charges related to tariff refunds, facility closures, and exits of businesses. We present adjusted EBITDA because we believe that adjusted EBITDA is a useful supplemental measure in evaluating the cash flows and performance of our business and provides greater transparency into our results of operations. Adjusted EBITDA is used by our management to perform such evaluations. Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for cash flow from operations, income from operations or other income statement measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. We believe that adjusted EBITDA is frequently used by investors, securities analysts and other interested parties in their evaluation of companies, many of which present adjusted EBITDA when reporting their results. Other companies may calculate adjusted EBITDA differently and it may not be comparable.
The reconciliations of these non-GAAP measures to the most directly comparable financial measures calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP are shown in the tables below.
Non-GAAP financial measures reflect adjustments based on the following items:
•Facility closures and business exits: we have excluded charges related to the closure of distribution and manufacturing facilities and our decisions to exit businesses as they represent infrequent transactions that impact the comparability between operating periods.
•Tariff refunds: we have excluded the impact of tariff refunds and the associated interest received for certain tariffs previously imposed under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act which were deemed unconstitutional. We believe the tariff refund amounts we have received represent infrequent transactions that impact the comparability between operating periods.
•Business contribution to partnership formation: we have excluded the gain related to the divestiture of the pet distribution business and its contribution to the formation of a partnership as it represents an infrequent transaction that impacts the comparability between operating periods.
•Substantially complete liquidation of a business in a foreign country: we have excluded the loss related to the cumulative foreign currency translation loss, previously recorded in Other Comprehensive Income (OCI), due to the substantially complete liquidation of Interpet as it represents an infrequent transaction that impacts the comparability between operating periods.
•Tax impact: adjustment represents the impact of the tax effect of the pre-tax non-GAAP adjustments excluded from non-GAAP net income. The tax impact of the non-GAAP adjustments is calculated based on the consolidated effective tax rate on a GAAP basis, applied to the non-GAAP adjustments.
From time to time in the future, there may be other items that we may exclude if we believe that doing so is consistent with the goal of providing useful supplemental information to investors and management.
We have not provided a reconciliation of non-GAAP measures to the corresponding GAAP measures on a forward-looking basis as we cannot do so without unreasonable efforts due to the potential variability and limited visibility of excluded items; these excluded items may include facility closures and exit costs, impairment charges and restructuring costs, among others.
