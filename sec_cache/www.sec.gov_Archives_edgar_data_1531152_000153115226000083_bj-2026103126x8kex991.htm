BJ’s Wholesale Club Holdings, Inc. Announces Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results
Strong third quarter results reflect accelerating comparable sales, robust traffic growth and record membership; full-year comparable sales and EPS guidance raised

Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Highlights
•Comparable club sales increased by 13.5% year-over-year
•Comparable club sales, excluding gasoline sales, increased by 4.1% year-over-year
•Membership fee income increased by 9.5% year-over-year to $138.4 million
•Digitally enabled comparable sales growth was 38%, reflecting two-year stacked comp growth of 69%
•Earnings per diluted share and adjusted earnings per diluted share(a) of $1.73
•The Company opened two new clubs and five new gas stations

Marlborough, Mass. (November 20, 2026) – BJ’s Wholesale Club Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: BJ) (the “Company”) today announced its financial results for the thirteen weeks and thirty-nine weeks ended October 31, 2026.

“We delivered a strong third quarter driven by robust traffic growth and record membership. Our 42nd birthday celebration was a resounding success. We seized a unique opportunity to invest in our stakeholders and to live our purpose of taking care of the families who depend on us. We’re excited about the momentum across our strategic priorities, and we remain committed to investing in growth for the long term,” said Bob Eddy, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, BJ’s Wholesale Club.

Additional Highlights:
•Total comparable club sales increased by 13.5% and 10.6% in the third quarter and first nine months of fiscal 2026, respectively, compared to the same periods in fiscal 2025. Excluding the impact of gasoline sales, comparable club sales increased by 4.1% and 2.9% in the third quarter and first nine months of fiscal 2026, respectively, compared to the same periods in fiscal 2025.
•Membership fee income increased to $138.4 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to $126.3 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025. Membership fee income increased to $406.3 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2026 compared to $370.0 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2025. The increase in both comparative periods was primarily driven by strength in membership acquisition, retention and higher-tier membership penetration across both new and existing clubs.
•Gross profit increased to $1.02 billion in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to $1.01 billion in the third quarter of fiscal 2025. Merchandise gross margin rate, which excludes gasoline sales and membership fee income, decreased by approximately 170 basis points compared to the third quarter of fiscal 2025 primarily driven by promotional activities, principally associated with the Company’s 42nd birthday celebration event. Gross profit increased to $3.16 billion in the first nine months of fiscal 2026 compared to $2.99 billion in the first nine months of fiscal 2025. Merchandise gross margin rate decreased by approximately 70 basis points compared to the first nine months of fiscal 2025 primarily driven by promotional activities associated with the Company’s 42nd birthday celebration event, partially offset by tariff refund benefits recognized in the first half of fiscal 2026.
•Selling, general and administrative expenses (“SG&A”) decreased to $696.1 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to $788.2 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025. SG&A increased to $2.35 billion in the first nine months of fiscal 2026 compared to $2.34 billion in the first nine months of fiscal 2025. SG&A in both periods included a benefit of approximately $240 million, net of associated legal fees, recognized in the third quarter of fiscal 2026, from a settlement to resolve litigation related to credit card interchange fees in which the Company was the plaintiff. The Company recognized an estimated $95 million of expenses related to the use of proceeds from the settlement, which partially offset the benefit. Gains from sale-leaseback transactions in the second and third quarters of fiscal 2026 also favorably impacted SG&A. Partially offsetting these favorable items were increases in labor, occupancy, and operational costs primarily as a result of new club and gas station openings.
•Income tax expense increased to $83.0 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to $56.0 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025. Income tax expense increased to $201.2 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2026 compared to $154.2 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2025.
•Net income increased to $219.5 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to $152.1 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025. Net income increased to $536.1 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2026 compared to $452.5 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2025.
•Adjusted EBITDA increased by 39.9% to $421.6 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to $301.4 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025. Adjusted EBITDA increased by 19.7% to $1.07 billion in the first nine months of fiscal 2026 compared to $891.1 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2025.
•Under its existing share repurchase program, the Company repurchased 376,942 shares of common stock, totaling $35.6 million, inclusive of associated costs, in the third quarter of fiscal 2026. In the first nine months of fiscal 2026, the Company repurchased 3,875,220 shares of common stock, totaling $366.3 million, inclusive of associated costs. Approximately $386.9 million remained available to purchase under such program.

Fiscal 2026 Ending January 30, 2027 Outlook

“Our third quarter reflects strong execution across the business, with comparable sales accelerating,” said Laura Felice, Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer, BJ’s Wholesale Club. “We invested meaningfully for our stakeholders while continuing to grow the business, and we are raising our full-year guidance for both comparable sales and adjusted EPS. With a strong balance sheet, we remain confident in our ability to deliver sustainable, profitable growth.”

•Comparable club sales, excluding the impact of gasoline sales, to increase 2.5% to 3.0% year-over-year

About BJ’s Wholesale Club Holdings, Inc.
BJ’s Wholesale Club Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: BJ) is a leading operator of membership warehouse clubs focused on delivering significant value to its members and serving a shared purpose: “We take care of the families who depend on us.” The Company provides a wide assortment of fresh foods, produce, a full-service deli, fresh bakery, household essentials and gas. In addition, BJ’s offers the latest technology, home decor, apparel, seasonal items and more to deliver unbeatable value to smart-saving families. Headquartered in Marlborough, Massachusetts, the Company pioneered the warehouse club model in New England in 1984 and currently operates 271 clubs and 211 BJ's Gas® locations in 22 states. For more information, please visit us at www.bjs.com.

Note Regarding Non-GAAP Financial Information
This press release includes financial measures that are not calculated in accordance with GAAP, including adjusted net income, adjusted net income per diluted share (“adjusted EPS”), adjusted EBITDA, adjusted free cash flow, net debt, net debt to last twelve months (“LTM”) adjusted EBITDA, and other key performance indicators, including comparable club sales, which includes all clubs that were open for at least 13 months at the beginning of the period and were in operation during the entirety of both periods being compared, including relocated clubs and expansions.
We define adjusted net income as net income as reported, adjusted for non-recurring, infrequent, or unusual charges, including restructuring charges, and other adjustments that the Company believes appropriate, net of the tax impact of such adjustments.
We define adjusted EPS as adjusted net income divided by the weighted-average diluted shares outstanding.
We define adjusted EBITDA as net income before interest expense, net, provision for income taxes, and depreciation and amortization, adjusted for the impact of certain other items, including stock-based compensation expense, restructuring, and other adjustments.
We define adjusted free cash flow as net cash provided by operating activities less additions to property and equipment, net of disposals, plus proceeds from sale-leaseback transactions.
We define net debt as total debt outstanding less cash and cash equivalents.
We define net debt to LTM adjusted EBITDA as net debt at the balance sheet date divided by adjusted EBITDA for the trailing twelve-month period.
We present adjusted net income, adjusted EPS, and adjusted EBITDA, which are not recognized financial measures under GAAP, because we believe such measures assist investors and analysts in comparing our operating performance across reporting periods on a consistent basis by excluding items that we do not believe are indicative of our core operating performance.
We believe that adjusted net income, adjusted EPS, and adjusted EBITDA are helpful in highlighting trends in our core operating performance compared to other measures, which can differ significantly depending on long-term strategic decisions regarding capital structure, the tax jurisdictions in which companies operate, and capital investments. We use adjusted net income, adjusted EPS, and adjusted EBITDA to supplement GAAP measures of performance in the evaluation of the effectiveness of our business strategies; to make budgeting decisions; and to compare our performance against that of other peer companies using similar measures. We also use adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EPS in connection with establishing annual and long-term incentive compensation.
We present adjusted free cash flow, which is not a recognized financial measure under GAAP, because we use it to report to our Board of Directors and we believe it assists investors and analysts in evaluating our liquidity. Adjusted free cash flow should not be considered as an alternative to cash flows from operations as a liquidity measure. We present net debt and net debt to LTM adjusted EBITDA, which are not recognized as financial measures under GAAP, because we use them to report to our Board of Directors and we believe they assist investors and analysts in evaluating our borrowing capacity. Net debt to LTM adjusted EBITDA is a key financial measure that is used by management to assess the borrowing capacity of the Company.
You are encouraged to evaluate these adjustments and the reasons we consider them appropriate for supplemental analysis. In evaluating adjusted net income, adjusted EPS, adjusted EBITDA, and net debt to LTM adjusted EBITDA, you should be aware that in the future we may incur expenses that are the same as or like some of the adjustments in our presentation of these metrics. Our presentation of adjusted net income, adjusted EPS, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted free cash flow, net debt, and net debt to LTM adjusted EBITDA should not be considered as alternatives to any other measure derived in accordance with GAAP and they should not be construed as an inference that the Company’s future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items. There can be no assurance that we will not modify the presentation of adjusted net income, adjusted EPS, adjusted EBITDA, or net debt to LTM adjusted EBITDA in the future, and any such modification may be material. In addition, adjusted net income, adjusted EPS, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted free cash flow, net debt, and net debt to LTM adjusted EBITDA may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies in our industry or across different industries.
