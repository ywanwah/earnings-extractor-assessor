GrabAGun Digital Holdings Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Gross Profit Increases 44% and Gross Margin Expands 480 Basis Points to 14.4%

 

Third Quarter Net Revenues Were $21.2 Million, a Decrease of 4.7% Year-Over-Year, Reflecting Lower Product Sales

 

Previously Announced Acquisition of Camfour Broadens GrabAGun’s Reach Across Digital Retail, Wholesale Distribution and Manufacturer Logistics

 

 

Coppell, Texas – November 12, 2026 – GrabAGun Digital Holdings Inc. (“GrabAGun,” “we,” “us,” “our,” or the “Company”) (NYSE: PEW), a technology-driven commerce, distribution, and logistics company serving the firearms, ammunition, and outdoor industry, today reported third quarter 2026 financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Marc Nemati, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of GrabAGun, commented, “Our third-quarter results reflect the progress we are making in improving the quality and profitability of our business. Gross profit increased 44% and gross margin expanded 480 basis points to 14.4%, primarily driven by average selling prices and supported by better sourcing, a stronger product cost basis and disciplined purchasing. Those improvements more than offset the impact of lower revenue, which reflected a difficult comparison to last year's elevated, IPO-driven demand.

 

"Subsequent to quarter end, we completed the acquisition of Camfour, a New England-based wholesale distributor funded entirely with cash on hand and without financing or issuing shares. We believe combining Camfour's distribution capabilities with our e-commerce and logistics platform will stimulate growth, expand our customer base and improve our purchasing economics.”

 

Mr. Nemati continued, "PEW Logistics continues to gain momentum, with SAR USA joining KelTec®, Derya Arms and Backwoods Suppressors as our fourth manufacturing customer. Last week, we launched KelTec's new website, which PEW Logistics now fully operates, selling KelTec firearms, accessories and apparel directly to consumers. This expands our role from fulfillment partner to full e-commerce operator, and we believe it creates a model we can extend to other manufacturers.

 

"With ample cash and minimal debt following the Camfour acquisition, we remain focused on disciplined investment in the areas that will drive long-term value: realizing the potential of Camfour, scaling PEW Logistics and strengthening the technology platform that we believe provides a durable competitive advantage. We will continue to evaluate every use of capital based on its ability to create long-term value for our shareholders."

 

Third Quarter Financial Highlights

 

•
Net revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $21.2 million, a decrease of 4.7% year-over-year, compared to $22.3 million in the prior-year quarter.
o
Firearms sales decreased 4.5% to $17.3 million.
o
Non-firearms sales decreased 11.8% to $3.7 million.
o
Service sales totaled $0.3 million; PEW Logistics, a wholly-owned subsidiary, did not have any revenue for the prior-year quarter.
•
Net revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $70.4 million, up 5.3% year-over-year, compared to $66.8 million in the same period of the prior year.
o
Firearms sales increased 5.0% to $58.3 million
o
Non-firearms sales increased 1.3% to $11.5 million
o
Service sales totaled $0.6 million; PEW Logistics did not have any revenue for the same period of the prior year.

 

 

•
Gross profit margin expanded 480 basis points to 14.4% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to a 9.6% gross profit margin in the prior year's quarter. Gross profit margin for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 expanded 280 basis points to 12.7% compared with gross profit margin of 9.9% in the prior year.
•
Loss from operations was $3.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to a loss from operations of $4.8 million in the same period of the prior year, with the improvement primarily driven by the increase in gross profit. Loss from operations was $9.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to a loss from operations of $3.9 million the prior year, driven primarily by public company expenses and increased personnel costs associated with headcount additions, offset partly by a decrease in stock-based compensation expense.
•
Net loss was $3.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to a net loss of $3.8 million in the prior-year quarter. Net loss was $6.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to a net loss of $2.9 million in the prior year.
•
Adjusted EBITDA1 totaled a loss of $2.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to a loss of $0.9 million in the prior year. Adjusted EBITDA1 totaled a loss of $5.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to income of $0.5 million in the prior year.
•
Cash and cash equivalents of $96.0 million, or $3.25 per share, with minimal debt, as of September 30, 2026.

About GrabAGun Digital Holdings Inc.

GrabAGun Digital Holdings Inc. (NYSE: PEW) is a technology-driven commerce, distribution, and logistics company serving the firearms, ammunition, and outdoor industry. The Company operates across three complementary capabilities: digital retail through GrabAGun.com, operated by its wholly-owned subsidiary GrabAGun LLC; wholesale distribution through its wholly-owned subsidiary Camfour LLC; and manufacturer logistics and platform services through its wholly-owned subsidiary PEW Logistics LLC. GrabAGun.com is one of the nation’s leading online firearms retailers, built on 16 years of proprietary technology spanning inventory and order management, pricing, demand forecasting, and regulatory compliance. PEW Logistics provides manufacturers with technology-enabled fulfillment, compliance, data, marketing, and direct-to-consumer capabilities. Together with Camfour, these separately operated businesses connect manufacturers, independent dealers, and consumers, with a goal of improving product access, distribution efficiency, and industry connectivity. For more information, visit investors.grabagun.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Information

 

We utilize Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin, non-GAAP financial measures, to supplement GAAP measures of performance as a tool to evaluate our historical financial and operational performance, identify trends affecting our business, and formulate business plans and make strategic decisions. We believe that Adjusted EBITDA provides users of our financial information with useful supplemental information that enables a better comparison of our performance across periods. We believe Adjusted EBITDA provides visibility to the underlying continuing operating performance by excluding the impact of interest income, net, income tax, and non-cash expenses, including depreciation, amortization, stock compensation, and certain costs, as management does not believe these to be representative of our core earnings. We also provide Adjusted EBITDA margin, which is calculated as Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue.

The non-GAAP financial measures have not been calculated in accordance with GAAP and should be considered in addition to results prepared in accordance with GAAP and should not be considered as a substitute for, or superior to, GAAP results. We caution investors that non-GAAP financial information, by its nature, departs from traditional accounting conventions. Adjusted EBITDA is not a liquidity measure and should not be considered as discretionary cash available to us to reinvest in the growth of our business or to distribute to shareholders or as a measure of cash that will be available to us to meet our obligations.

 

We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) excluding interest income, net, income tax, and non-cash expenses, including depreciation and amortization, stock-based compensation, and certain non-recurring costs. Adjusted EBITDA margin represents Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of revenue.
