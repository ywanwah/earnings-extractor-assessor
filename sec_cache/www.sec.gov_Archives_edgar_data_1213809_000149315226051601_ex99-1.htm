DYADIC ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS AND RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

 

Advances Commercialization, Expands Strategic Partnerships and Accelerates Biopharmaceutical Platform Validation

 

Dyadic to host earnings call on November 12, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. ET

 

JUPITER, FL — November 12, 2026 — Dyadic International, Inc. (NASDAQ: DYAI), doing business as Dyadic Applied BioSolutions (the “Company” or “Dyadic”), a biotechnology company developing and commercializing precision-engineered, animal-free recombinant proteins and enzymes, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Highlights included the Company’s first direct commercial protein sales, broader third-party distribution, additional partner-commercialized products, new strategic development opportunities and progress across its C1 and Dapibus™ production platforms.

 

“During the third quarter, we reached a key commercialization milestone for our animal-free protein portfolio, advancing market adoption through initial product sales and growing customer engagement across the food, life sciences, and biopharmaceutical sectors,” said Joe Hazelton, President and Chief Operating Officer. “While early sales remain modest and evaluation timelines are longer than anticipated, we are seeing increased sampling requests—particularly for transferrin—as we convert initial qualifications into potential business.

 

“Concurrently, partner-led commercialization is building momentum. Proliant is expanding promotion for C1-produced AlbuFree™ DX with additional products planned, Inzymes commercialized a second Dapibus-produced chymosin, and our alpha-lactalbumin collaborations are moving toward commercial-scale supply. Looking ahead, we are placing greater emphasis on larger strategic partnerships and collaborations, licensing and supply arrangements, and other potential transactions involving our platforms, products, and commercial programs. We continue to evaluate all options to optimize the value of our platforms and assets, including financing alternatives and other opportunities aimed at strengthening our balance sheet, accelerating commercialization and maximizing shareholder value,” Mr. Hazelton added.

 

Recent Company Developments

 

Commercial Products & Market Adoption

 

First Direct Commercial Product Sales: During the third quarter, Dyadic recorded its first direct commercial product sales, primarily of recombinant transferrin and DNase I. Although initial sales were not material in amount, they are a milestone as they establish a direct-product revenue channel, alongside existing and potential partner-generated license, milestone and profit-sharing or royalty economics.

 

Expanded OEM Distribution: Integrated Biotherapeutics (IBT Bioservices) launched an initial portfolio of six Dyadic-produced research-use-only recombinant protein products through its distribution network. The companies are evaluating additional offerings, extending Dyadic’s potential reach across research, diagnostics, reagent and cell culture applications.

 

Growing Customer Engagement: Sampling requests and customer evaluations increased for human and bovine transferrin and other recombinant proteins. Dyadic is supporting customer testing and qualification with the objective of converting these engagements into repeat purchases and sustained product demand.

 

Proliant AlbuFree™ Product Commercialization and Expansion: Proliant Health & Biologicals continued commercial promotion of AlbuFree™ DX, an animal-free recombinant human serum albumin produced using Dyadic’s C1 platform, including performance data relevant to diagnostic assay blocking and protein stabilization. Proliant also presented analytical research reporting high purity, low endotoxin levels and molecular characteristics resembling native human serum albumin. In addition, Proliant outlined planned additions to the AlbuFree™ family: AlbuFree™ TX for cell culture media and AlbuFree™ CGT for cell and gene therapy applications. These planned extensions offer opportunities to broaden C1-enabled product adoption and potential recurring commercial revenue.

 

Second Commercial Chymosin Demonstrates Dapibus™ Versatility: In November 2026, Inzymes reported that the first commercial sale of the Company’s second chymosin product, camel chymosin produced with the Company’s Dapibus™ production strain, occurred in October 2026, achieving the first-commercial-sale milestone under the Inzymes agreement. The Company expects to receive the $225,000 milestone payment and recognize it as revenue in the fourth quarter of 2026.

 

 
 

 

Strategic Partnerships, Licensing & Product Development

 

Advancing Alpha-Lactalbumin Toward Commercialization: Advanced collaboration activities with BRIG BIO and Strive Nutrition to develop, scale and potentially commercialize Dapibus-produced bovine alpha-lactalbumin for next-generation nutrition products. The participants bring complementary protein development, manufacturing, regulatory and commercialization capabilities intended to support economically competitive commercial-scale production.

 

Expanding Strategic Licensing Opportunities: Continued discussions with additional global companies regarding potential licensing, development and commercial arrangements involving alpha-lactalbumin and other animal-free proteins, as well as product collaborations and broader strategic opportunities involving C1, Dapibus and the Company’s recombinant protein portfolio. These discussions are intended to leverage partners’ resources, manufacturing capabilities and market access to advance higher-value products, including alpha-lactalbumin and transferrin, while creating opportunities for upfront fees, milestones and ongoing commercial participation.

 

Broader Nutrition Protein Portfolio: Initiated development of recombinant bovine lactoferrin, complementing the Company’s human lactoferrin and alpha-lactalbumin programs and extending its portfolio of animal-free nutritional and functional proteins.

 

C1 Biopharmaceutical Platform – Advancing Global Vaccine and Antibody Programs

 

Rapid Biologics Development: Demonstrated C1’s rapid development capabilities by producing and purifying two Scripps Research-designed Bundibugyo ebolavirus antigens in approximately 15 days from plasmid to C-tag-purified protein. Dyadic also demonstrated the development of a clonal C1 production strain and production of a Protein A-purified monoclonal antibody in approximately seven weeks. In our view, these achievements highlight C1’s potential to accelerate vaccine and antibody development, shorten manufacturing timelines, reduce production costs and enable faster advancement of biologics toward preclinical and clinical evaluation.

 

Gates Foundation Programs Advance to Animal Studies: Under its approximately $3.1 million Gates Foundation-funded program, Dyadic achieved production yields of approximately 12.1 g/L for RSV monoclonal antibody RSM01 and 13.2 g/L for malaria monoclonal antibody MAM01 in seven-day fermentation runs. Independent laboratory evaluations included binding studies of both C1-produced antibodies conducted by Duke University and neutralization testing of RSM01 conducted by IQVIA. Following quarter-end, the Gates Medical Research Institute initiated preclinical animal studies in October 2026 to evaluate the pharmacokinetics of the C1-produced RSV and malaria antibodies through direct, head-to-head comparisons with their respective GMP CHO-produced counterparts. Results are anticipated in the first quarter of 2027. These studies aim to generate additional comparative preclinical data to support the antibodies’ continued advancement toward potential clinical development. We believe the demonstrated production yields, together with C1’s ability to produce antibodies with targeted human-like glycosylation profiles, further highlight the platform’s potential to manufacture complex biologics efficiently while reducing production costs and increasing manufacturing flexibility.

 

Accelerating C1 Biopharmaceutical Validation and Adoption: Continued to advance multiple vaccine and monoclonal antibody programs through preclinical development, including ongoing animal studies evaluating ebolavirus and other vaccine candidates conducted in collaboration with Fondazione Biotecnopolo di Siena (FBS) and the European Vaccines Hub (EVH). Dyadic also continued advancing programs with Scripps Research, CEPI, NIAID/NIH, the University of Oxford, Wits University, the Israel Institute for Biological Research and other collaborators, including participation in the €170 million European Vaccines Hub initiative (representing total initiative funding, not funding awarded to Dyadic). These ongoing studies and collaborations are expected to generate additional preclinical and technical data to further validate C1’s capabilities and support broader adoption of the platform for biopharmaceutical applications addressing infectious and other diseases. Collectively, we believe these advances reinforce C1’s potential to accelerate the development and manufacturing of vaccines and therapeutics for pandemic preparedness and traditional medicine, reduce production costs and expand global access to biologic medicines.

 

 
 

 

Financial Highlights

 

Cash Position: As of September 30, 2026, cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and the carrying value of investment-grade securities, including accrued interest, were $6,046,083 compared to $8,587,289 as of December 31, 2025.

 

Revenue: Total revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $449,101, representing a decrease of $715,516 or 61.4% compared to $1,164,617 for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was driven by a $538,199 decrease in grant revenue from lower activities under the Gates Foundation and CEPI grants and a $187,728 decrease in research and development revenue resulting from the completion of several collaboration projects in the prior-year period, partially offset by a $10,411 increase in license fee revenue.

 

Cost of Revenue: Total cost of revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $375,115, representing a decrease of $648,888 or 63.4% compared to $1,024,003 for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was due to a $503,610 decrease in cost of grant revenue from lower activities under the Gates Foundation and CEPI grants and a $145,278 decrease in the cost of research and development revenue.

 

R&D Expenses: Research and development expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $429,945, a decrease of $141,927 or 24.8% compared to $571,872 for the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily due to a $199,105 decrease in third-party contract research services resulting from a reduction in the number of active internal research initiatives, partially offset by a $57,178 increase in personnel costs and other costs.

 

G&A Expenses: General and administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $1,450,750, a decrease of $30,606 or 2.1%, compared to $1,481,356 for the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower legal and professional expenses of $166,541, partially offset by higher outside business development services and other expenses of $146,500.

 

Loss from Operations: Loss from operations for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $1,812,365, a decrease of $113,004 or 5.9%, compared to $1,925,369 for the same period in 2025. The decrease was largely attributable to lower total cost of revenue of $648,888, lower research and development expenses of $141,927, and lower general and administrative expenses of $30,606, partially offset by lower total revenue of $715,516.

 

Other Income (Expenses), Net: For the three months ended September 30, 2026, total other income, net, was $455,324, compared to total other expenses, net, of $50,643 for the same period in 2025. The change was primarily due to a $545,527 non-cash gain from the change in fair value of the warrant liability, net of $388,702 of issuance costs allocated to the warrant liability, partially offset by lower interest income of $44,140.

 

Net Loss: Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $1,357,041 or $(0.04) per share, compared to $1,976,012 or $(0.06) per share for the same period a year ago.

About Dyadic Applied BioSolutions

 

Dyadic Applied BioSolutions is a global biotechnology company that uses its proprietary microbial platforms to produce recombinant proteins that are sold or licensed to partners across the life sciences, food and nutrition, and bio-industrial markets. These high-quality proteins are designed to enable customers to develop more efficient, scalable, and sustainable products. Dyadic’s Dapibus™ and C1 expression systems support flexible, cost-effective manufacturing, and are the foundation of a growing portfolio of commercial and partnered programs.
