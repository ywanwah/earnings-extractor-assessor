Moderna Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Updates
Reports third quarter revenue of $1.3 billion, GAAP net income of $0.2 billion and GAAP diluted EPS of $0.38

Increases 2026 expected revenue growth to 15%–25%, exceeding the previous expectation of up to 10% growth

Improves 2026 GAAP operating expense outlook by approximately $0.2 billion from previous estimate

Increases 2026 expected ending cash balance to $8.1–$8.3 billion

Highlights positive Phase 3 data for intismeran and U.S. FDA approval of mFLUSIVA, the Company's fifth approved product globally

CAMBRIDGE, MA / ACCESSWIRE / November 6, 2026 / Moderna, Inc. (NASDAQ:MRNA) today reported financial results and provided business updates for the third quarter of 2026.
“We delivered 29% revenue growth in the third quarter, alongside the U.S. approval of mFLUSIVA, our fifth approved product globally, and positive Phase 3 results for intismeran in melanoma,” said Stéphane Bancel, Chief Executive Officer of Moderna. “Building on this momentum, we increased our full-year revenue growth outlook to 15%-25%, while continuing to reduce costs and strengthen our balance sheet. Looking ahead, we anticipate additional intismeran readouts, pivotal data in propionic acidemia and further regulatory approvals as we advance our pipeline and build a more diversified company.”

Commercial Strategy

Moderna continued to execute its multi-year revenue growth strategy during the third quarter, supported by the launch of updated COVID vaccines, expansion of its approved respiratory vaccine portfolio and execution of international strategic partnerships with the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia. In September, the Company signed a five-year agreement with a local manufacturer in Mexico for COVID-19 vaccines, supporting recurring revenue and international market expansion.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Revenue: Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 increased 29% to $1.3 billion, compared to $1.0 billion in the same period in 2025, primarily driven by higher COVID vaccine sales in international markets, supported by deliveries under long-term strategic government partnerships. Revenue was $780 million in the U.S. and $533 million in international markets.

Cost of Sales: Cost of sales for the third quarter of 2026 was $318 million, including $65 million of third-party royalties. Cost of sales increased by 54% compared to the same period in 2025, primarily driven by higher product sales volume and other manufacturing-related costs.
1



Research and Development Expenses: Research and development expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $583 million, a 27% decrease compared to the same period in 2025, primarily driven by lower clinical development costs following the completion and wind-down of certain late-stage studies in the Company's infectious disease portfolio, as well as continued cost efficiencies across the organization.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses: Selling, general and administrative expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $282 million, a 5% increase year-over-year. We continue to maintain cost discipline across the organization.

Income Taxes: Income tax provisions for both periods were not material, as the Company continues to maintain a global valuation allowance against most of its deferred tax assets.

Net Income: Net income was $162 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a net loss of $(200) million in the third quarter of 2025, representing a $362 million improvement year-over-year.

Earnings Per Share: Diluted earnings per share were $0.38 for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a loss per share of $(0.51) for the third quarter of 2025.

Cash Position: Cash, cash equivalents and investments as of September 30, 2026, were $8.0 billion, compared to $6.9 billion as of June 30, 2026. The increase primarily reflected $2.6 billion in net proceeds from the issuance of convertible notes and related capped call transactions, partially offset by the $950 million payment related to the litigation settlement announced in the first quarter of 2026, and cash used in operations, including the seasonal working capital impact from third-quarter product sales, the majority of which remained uncollected at quarter-end.

2026 Financial Framework
Revenue: The Company now expects total revenue growth of 15% to 25% in 2026, exceeding its previous outlook of up to 10% growth. Moderna continues to expect approximately half of its 2026 revenue to come from the United States and half from international markets.

Cost of Sales: Cost of sales for 2026 is expected to be approximately $1.7 billion, including the previously announced $0.9 billion non-recurring litigation settlement charge.

Research and Development Expenses: Research and development expenses for 2026 are expected to be approximately $2.7 billion, reduced from the Company's previous expectation of approximately $2.9 billion.

Selling, General and Administrative Expenses: Selling, general and administrative expenses for 2026 are expected to be approximately $1.0 billion, unchanged from previous forecast.

Income Taxes: The Company continues to expect negligible income tax expense for 2026.

Capital Expenditures: Capital expenditures for 2026 are expected to be approximately $0.2 billion, improved from the previous expectation of $0.2 billion to $0.3 billion.
2



Cash and Investments: The Company now expects year-end 2026 cash and investments of approximately $8.1 billion to $8.3 billion, increased from its previous outlook of $4.7 billion to $5.2 billion. The increase reflects improved operational execution and proceeds from the Company’s convertible notes financing. This outlook excludes additional drawdowns from the approximately $0.9 billion remaining available under the Company's credit facility.
Recent Progress and Upcoming Late-Stage Pipeline Milestones
Infectious Disease Vaccines

Seasonal Influenza Vaccine (mRNA-1010): In August, Moderna received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval for mFLUSIVA®, its seasonal influenza vaccine, for adults aged 50–64 years and accelerated approval for adults aged 65 years and older, marking the Company's fifth approved product globally. Moderna is conducting a limited U.S. launch for the 2026–2027 influenza season, with approval occurring after the annual contracting cycle. The Company also received approval for mFLUSIVA in Canada.

Flu/COVID Combination Vaccine (mRNA-1083): Moderna received regulatory approval in Canada for mCOFLU®, the Company's flu/COVID combination vaccine, following approval of the vaccine under the brand name mCOMBRIAX® in the European Union. The vaccine remains under regulatory review in additional markets, including Japan, the United Kingdom and Australia. Moderna continues to await further guidance from the FDA regarding refiling in the United States.

Norovirus Vaccine (mRNA-1403): Following the Phase 3 interim analysis, which did not meet the statistical criteria for early success, Moderna does not plan to initiate an additional cohort this season. The Company intends to engage with health authorities to determine the program's path forward.

Oncology Therapeutics

Intismeran autogene (mRNA-4157): In October, Moderna and Merck presented positive Phase 3 results from the INTerpath-001 study in resected high-risk melanoma at the European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) Congress 2026. Intismeran autogene in combination with KEYTRUDA® (pembrolizumab) reduced the risk of recurrence or death by 42% (HR=0.58) and the risk of distant metastasis or death by 38% (HR=0.62), compared with KEYTRUDA alone, in the intention-to-treat population. The benefit was greater after six months, following the intismeran immunization regimen, as intismeran plus KEYTRUDA reduced the risk of recurrence or death by 58% (piecewise HR=0.42 [95% CI, 0.30–0.58]) compared to KEYTRUDA alone in a pre-specified sensitivity analysis. Moderna and Merck are working toward regulatory submissions and preparing for a potential commercial launch, subject to regulatory approval.

mRNA-4359: Moderna continues Phase 2 clinical trial of its investigational cancer antigen therapy in first-line metastatic melanoma, previously treated metastatic melanoma and first-line non-small cell lung cancer.

mRNA-2808: Abstract accepted for oral presentation at American Society of Hematology meeting on December 12th in New Orleans.
3



mRNA-4203: Moderna and Immatics continue dosing in a Phase 1 study evaluating mRNA-4203 in combination with anzu-cel (anzutresgene autoleucel, IMA203).

Rare Disease Therapeutics

Propionic Acidemia (mRNA-3927): The registrational study has reached its target enrollment, with pivotal data expected in 2026.

Methylmalonic Acidemia (mRNA-3705): Moderna has deferred a decision on initiating a pivotal study until the propionic acidemia registrational study readout.

Moderna Corporate Updates

•Leadership Appointment: In September, Moderna announced the appointment of Juan Andres as Chief Operating Officer, effective October 5, 2026. Mr. Andres previously served in senior leadership positions at Moderna and will oversee the Company's operational strategy and execution, including its global manufacturing organization.
•Leadership Retirement: In September, Moderna announced that Jerh Collins, Ph.D., Chief Technical Operations and Quality Officer, retired following four years with the Company.
•Financing: In September, Moderna completed a convertible senior notes offering totaling approximately $3.0 billion in principal amount. After accounting for the purchase of capped calls designed to mitigate potential future dilution and associated fees, we raised approximately $2.6 billion in net proceeds. The financing provides additional financial flexibility to support continued investment in oncology and address future debt obligations.
•Litigation Settlement: Moderna and GSK entered into an agreement on October 28, 2026, to settle all disputes between the parties which were pending in the U.S. and Europe. Pursuant to the agreement, the parties granted patent cross-licenses in the field of infectious disease and are not obligated to make payments of any kind in connection with such licenses.

About Moderna

Moderna is a pioneer and leader in the field of mRNA medicine. Through the advancement of its technology platform, Moderna is reimagining how medicines are made to transform how we treat and prevent diseases. Since its founding, Moderna's mRNA platform has enabled the development of vaccines and therapeutics across infectious diseases, cancer, rare diseases and more.

With a global team and a unique culture, driven by the Company's values and mindsets, Moderna's mission is to deliver the greatest possible impact to people through mRNA medicines.
