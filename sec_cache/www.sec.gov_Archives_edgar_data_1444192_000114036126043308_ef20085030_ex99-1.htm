Grace Therapeutics Announces Second Quarter 2027 Financial Results,
Provides Business Update

Princeton, N.J., November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Grace Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: GRCE) (Grace Therapeutics or the Company), a late-stage biopharma company advancing GTx-104, a novel, injectable formulation of nimodipine that has completed a Phase 3 clinical trial and is being developed for IV infusion to address significant unmet medical needs in aSAH patients, today announced the financial results and business highlights for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

“With no clinical safety or efficacy deficiencies identified in the U.S. Food and Drug Administration’s (FDA) Complete Response Letter (CRL), we continue to execute our plan to address the chemistry, manufacturing and controls (CMC) and non-clinical data items, and are making progress toward resubmission of our New Drug Application (NDA) for GTx-104,” said Prashant Kohli, Chief Executive Officer of Grace Therapeutics. “Our dual-source manufacturing strategy to address potential remediation issues at our current contract manufacturer is progressing, and the timing of our NDA resubmission will reflect whichever manufacturing site reaches readiness first. The financing concluded in August extends our expected cash runway through the end of calendar 2028, and positions us well as we work toward NDA resubmission. We look forward to reporting our progress as we work toward achieving these important milestones.”

Second Quarter 2027 and Recent Corporate Highlights


 •
Completed a $10 million private placement financing with certain institutional and accredited investors. The Company intends to use the net proceeds from the financing to support the manufacturing and regulatory work needed to advance GTx-104.


•
Announced receipt of the FDA meeting minutes from a Type A Meeting held with the FDA to discuss the CRL issued on April 23, 2026. The FDA did not identify any clinical safety or efficacy deficiencies and did not request additional clinical data. The Company is working on its previously disclosed plan to address the identified issues and will report progress as key milestones are achieved.


•
Initiated a dual-source manufacturing strategy for GTx-104 to address potential remediation issues from its current contract manufacturing organization (CMO). The Company’s current CMO has responded to the FDA, submitted a corrective action plan, and, the Company believes, is executing that plan in direct communication with the FDA. Reinspection timing depends on the current CMO’s readiness and FDA scheduling. In parallel, technology transfer was initiated during the quarter ended September 30, 2026 to a second, U.S.-based CMO, starting with analytical method transfer. The Company has also initiated the required non-clinical studies. The NDA resubmission may be supported by either CMO, or both, and its timing will reflect the pathway that reaches readiness first.
 
Second Quarter 2027 Financial Results
 
The Company reported a net loss of $2.1 million, or $0.11 per share, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of $1.2 million from the net loss of $0.9 million, or $0.06 per share, for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in net loss was primarily due to a $1.4 million favorable change in the fair value of derivative warrant liabilities recorded during the three months ended September 30, 2025 with no such activity recorded in the three months ended September 30, 2026 as the applicable warrants have expired, as well as an increase in research and development expenses of $0.3 million, offset partially by a $0.6 million decrease in general and administrative expenses.

Total research and development expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $0.9 million, compared to $0.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase of $0.3 million primarily reflected higher third-party research activities related to the dual-source manufacturing strategy for GTx-104 and the required non-clinical studies identified in the CRL.

General and administrative expenses were $1.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, a decrease of $0.6 million from $2.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily the result of a $0.3 million decrease in professional fees primarily related to decreased legal fees, a $0.1 million decrease in pre-commercial planning activities for GTx-104, and a $0.1 million decrease in salaries and benefits.

Cash Runway

As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents were $21.3 million, a net increase of $4.3 million compared to cash and cash equivalents of $17.0 million at March 31, 2026.  Cash used in operations for the six months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $5.0 million.  As announced in August 2026, the Company completed a private placement of its common stock with certain institutional and accredited investors. Net proceeds to the Company were $9.2 million. The Company plans to use its existing cash and cash equivalents towards resolving the items cited in the FDA’s CRL, working capital and other general corporate purposes. The Company believes its existing cash and cash equivalents will be sufficient to sustain planned operations, including the activities to address the items cited in the CRL, through the end of calendar 2028.

About aneurysmal Subarachnoid Hemorrhage (aSAH)

aSAH is bleeding over the surface of the brain in the subarachnoid space between the brain and the skull, which contains blood vessels that supply the brain. A primary cause of such bleeding is the rupture of an aneurysm in the brain. The result is aSAH, a relatively uncommon type of stroke that accounts for about 5% of all strokes and an estimated 42,500 U.S. hospital treated patients per year.

About GTx-104

GTx-104 is a novel, injectable formulation of nimodipine that has completed a Phase 3 clinical trial and is being developed for IV infusion in aSAH patients to address significant unmet medical needs. The unique nanoparticle technology of GTx-104 facilitates aqueous formulation of insoluble nimodipine for a standard peripheral IV infusion. GTx-104 provides a convenient IV delivery of nimodipine in the Intensive Care Unit potentially eliminating the need for nasogastric tube administration in unconscious or dysphagic patients. Intravenous delivery of GTx-104 also has the potential to lower food effects, drug-to-drug interactions, and eliminate potential dosing errors. Further, GTx-104 has the potential to better manage hypotension in aSAH patients. GTx-104 has been administered in over 200 patients and healthy volunteers and was well tolerated with significantly lower inter- and intra-subject pharmacokinetic variability compared to nimodipine oral capsules.

About Grace Therapeutics

Grace Therapeutics, Inc. (Grace Therapeutics or the Company) is a late-stage biopharma company with drug candidates addressing rare and orphan diseases. Grace Therapeutics’ novel drug delivery technologies have the potential to improve the performance of currently marketed drugs by achieving faster onset of action, enhanced efficacy, reduced side effects, and more convenient drug delivery. Grace Therapeutics’ lead clinical asset, GTx-104, is an IV infusion targeting aneurysmal Subarachnoid Hemorrhage (aSAH), a rare and life-threatening medical emergency in which bleeding occurs over the surface of the brain in the subarachnoid space between the brain and skull. GTx-104 has been granted Orphan Drug Designation by the FDA, which provides seven years of marketing exclusivity post-launch in the United States if certain conditions are met at NDA approval, and additional intellectual property protection with 52 granted and pending patents.
