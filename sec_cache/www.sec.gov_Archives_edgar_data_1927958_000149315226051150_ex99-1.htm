AIRO Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

MCLEAN, Va. - AIRO Group Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: AIRO) (“AIRO” or the “Company”), a global leader in advanced aerospace and defense technologies, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

(All results compared to prior-year comparative period, unless otherwise noted)

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Revenue of $17.5 million increased 179% compared to revenue of $6.3 million
   
●Gross margin of 57% improved versus 44%
   
●Operating loss of ($13.1) million versus operating loss of ($12.0) million
   
●Net loss of ($12.0) million versus net loss of ($8.0) million
   
●Adjusted EBITDA of $(7.5) million compared to $(8.0) million(1)
   
●Drone backlog of $200 million, which includes U.S. opportunities, as of November 5, 2026
   
●Total opportunity pipeline of $1.5 billion as of November 5, 2026

 

(1) Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures and Backlog” below.

 

“Demand for reliable, mission-critical drone capabilities continues to accelerate, and AIRO is well positioned to capture this opportunity,” said Joe Burns, Chief Executive Officer of AIRO. “During the quarter, we secured our first U.S. order, advanced the RQ-70 toward production, and continued building a more integrated and diversified unmanned systems platform. These accomplishments, together with the operational synergies we are realizing across the business, reinforce our confidence in AIRO’s long-term growth strategy.”

 

“With a growing Drone backlog, increasing customer engagement across U.S. and allied defense markets, and continued progress across our strategic growth initiatives, we believe AIRO is entering its next phase of growth from a position of strength,” stated Dr. Chirinjeev Kathuria, Executive Chairman.

●Revenue increased 179% to $17.5 million, driven by stronger-than-expected growth in the Drones segment, partially offset by continued softness in the Avionics and Training businesses. Third quarter 2025 results were adversely impacted by lower drone shipment volumes, creating a favorable comparison against the prior-year period.
   
●Gross profit was $9.9 million, or 57% of revenue. Gross margin expanded significantly year over year, reflecting a favorable mix shift toward higher-volume drone sales and improved operating efficiencies.
   
●Loss from operations and net loss were $(13.1) million and $(12.0) million, respectively. Profitability was impacted by higher operating expenses associated with investments to scale the business and support the Company’s long-term growth objectives.
   
●Adjusted EBITDA was $(7.5) million, compared to $(8.0) million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA improved year over year, driven by improved revenue performance partially offset by higher operating expenses related to investment to scale the business.

 

Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and Capital Allocation

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company held $37.8 million of cash and only $1.2 million of debt, positioning it to fund ongoing strategic and growth initiatives. Accounts receivable decreased by $29.6 million during the quarter, primarily driven by the receipt of customer payments associated with Drone deliveries completed in prior periods.

 

Outlook

 

●The Company reiterates its expectation for full-year 2026 revenue growth of 15% to 25% year over year and now expects growth to be at the lower end of the stated range.
   
●The Company also reiterates its full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance in the negative mid- to high-teens million-dollar range.
   
●Drone backlog, including U.S. opportunities, totaled approximately $200 million as of November 5, 2026. The Company expects the majority of this backlog to convert to revenue over the next 12 months, providing strong visibility into future growth. As is typical for businesses serving government and defense customers, revenue recognition may vary meaningfully across quarters depending on contract timing, production schedules and delivery milestones.
   
●As of November 5, 2026, the Company’s total opportunity pipeline totaled approximately $1.5 billion, reflecting strong customer demand and expanding opportunities across U.S. and allied defense markets.

About AIRO

 

AIRO Group Holdings is a next-generation aerospace and defense platform driving innovation across defense and commercial markets. Headquartered in McLean, VA, with operations in the U.S., Canada, and Denmark, AIRO combines a global reach with deep technical expertise.

 

Through a vertically integrated model, AIRO delivers mission-critical solutions centered on its drone platforms, leveraging advanced avionics, integrated training capabilities, and embedded autonomy across systems.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures and Backlog

 

To supplement its condensed consolidated financial statements prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP, AIRO uses EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA margin, as described below, to facilitate analysis of its financial and business trends and for internal planning and forecasting purposes. AIRO defines (1) EBITDA as net loss before depreciation and amortization, income tax (benefit) expense, net, and interest (income) expense, net, (2) Adjusted EBITDA as net loss before depreciation and amortization, income tax (benefit) expense, interest (income) expense, net, gain on extinguishment of debt, stock-based compensation, strategic workforce transition costs, contingent consideration fair value adjustments, warrant fair value adjustment, and IPO contingencies, and (3) Adjusted EBITDA margin as Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue. The above items are excluded from EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA because these items are either non-cash in nature, or because the amount and timing of these items is unpredictable, or because they are not driven by core results of operations, thereby rendering comparisons with prior periods and competitors less meaningful. AIRO believes Adjusted EBITDA provides useful information to investors and others in understanding and evaluating its results of operations, as well as provides useful measures for period-to-period comparisons of its business performance. Moreover, Adjusted EBITDA is a key measurement used by AIRO management internally to make operating decisions, including those related to analyzing operating expenses, evaluating performance and performing strategic planning and annual budgeting.

 

There are limitations associated with the use of non-GAAP financial measures. These non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered as alternatives to performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP. AIRO’s presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures should not be construed to imply that its future results will be unaffected by items that are excluded from these metrics. In addition, AIRO’s definitions of these non-GAAP financial measures may be different from similarly titled non-GAAP measures used by other companies. These non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as an analytical tool and you should not consider any of these non-GAAP financial measures in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. See the tables that follow for a reconciliation of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA to net loss and Adjusted EBITDA Margin to net loss margin, the most directly comparable financial measures stated in accordance with GAAP.

Drones segment backlog amounts outside of the United States were translated to U.S. dollars using applicable exchange rates as of market close on November 5, 2026, and may increase or decrease based on fluctuations in foreign exchange rates. Beginning with the third quarter of 2026, AIRO will include U.S. backlog in its reported Drones segment backlog to reflect the ramp-up of its U.S. manufacturing efforts.
