RXO Delivers Strong Third-Quarter Results with Profitable Growth and Continued Market Share Gains

•Profit exceeded outlook, primarily driven by continued Brokerage outperformance.
•Brokerage full-truckload volume improved every month and grew by 3% year over year; gross profit per load increased by 31% sequentially, driven by spot mix of 51%.
•Strength across Complementary Services, including 5% year-over-year stop growth in Last Mile and ~$140 million of freight under management awarded to Managed Transportation.
•Announced that C.H. Robinson has agreed to purchase RXO; transaction expected to close in the first half of 2027.

CHARLOTTE, N.C. – October 29, 2026 – RXO (NYSE: RXO) today reported strong third-quarter financial results.

RXO Chairman and CEO Drew Wilkerson said, “We exceeded the high end of our third-quarter profit outlook, demonstrating the momentum we’re building across RXO. We continued to take market share in Brokerage, and achieved a 3 percent increase in truckload volume, outperforming the market. We achieved a 900 basis-point sequential increase in truckload spot mix, which helped drive the best sequential improvement in gross profit per load in more than four years. Complementary Services also contributed to our strong results, with Last Mile stops up 5 percent. Additionally, Managed Transportation was awarded approximately $140 million in freight under management.”

Wilkerson continued, “As we move toward the pending acquisition by C.H. Robinson, our priorities remain unchanged. We’re staying close to our customers, listening to their needs and helping them navigate an evolving freight market. Our team remains focused on delivering the best service, solutions, innovation and relationships in the industry, and we have strong momentum heading into the fourth quarter.”

Companywide Results

RXO’s revenue was $1.9 billion for the third quarter, compared to $1.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025. Gross margin was 14.6%, compared to 16.5% in the third quarter of 2025.

The company reported third-quarter 2026 GAAP net income of $3 million, compared to a net loss of $14 million in the third quarter of 2025. The third-quarter 2026 GAAP net income included $7 million in transaction, integration, restructuring and other costs. Adjusted net income in the quarter was $16 million, compared to adjusted net income of $2 million in the third quarter of 2025.

Adjusted EBITDA was $51 million, compared to $32 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 2.7%, compared to 2.3% in the third quarter of 2025.

GAAP earnings per share were impacted $0.07, net of tax, by transaction, integration, restructuring and other costs, and amortization of intangibles. For the third quarter, RXO reported GAAP diluted earnings per share of $0.02. Adjusted diluted earnings per share was $0.09.

Brokerage

Volume in RXO’s Brokerage business increased by 2% year over year in the third quarter. Truckload volume improved every month throughout the quarter and increased by 3%. Less-than-truckload volume increased by 2%.

Truckload spot mix was 51% of volume in the quarter, up from 29% in the third quarter of 2025 and up from 42% in the second quarter of 2026. This helped drive the largest sequential gross profit per load growth rate in more than four years.

Brokerage gross margin was 12.6% in the third quarter.

Complementary Services

Managed Transportation was awarded approximately $140 million of freight under management in the third quarter.

Last Mile stops increased by 5% year over year.

RXO’s complementary services gross margin was 18.7% for the quarter.

C.H. Robinson Transaction

As announced on October 5, 2026, RXO entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by C.H. Robinson in a stock-and-cash transaction for an implied value of $5.8 billion as of the announcement. The transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory and shareholder approval.

More details and discussion of RXO’s financial performance can be found in the quarterly report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Given the proposed transaction with C.H. Robinson, RXO will not be hosting an earnings conference call.

About RXO

RXO (NYSE: RXO) is a leading provider of asset-light transportation solutions. RXO offers tech-enabled truck brokerage services together with complementary solutions including managed transportation and last mile delivery. The company combines massive capacity and cutting-edge technology to move freight efficiently through supply chains across North America. The company is headquartered in Charlotte, N.C. Non-GAAP Financial Measures
We provide reconciliations of the non-GAAP financial measures contained in this release to the most directly comparable measure under GAAP, which are set forth in the financial tables attached to this release.
The non-GAAP financial measures in this release include: adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (“adjusted EBITDA”); adjusted EBITDA margin; and adjusted net income and adjusted diluted income per share (“adjusted EPS”).
We believe that these adjusted financial measures facilitate analysis of our ongoing business operations because they exclude items that may not reflect, or are unrelated to, RXO’s core operating performance, and may assist investors with comparisons to prior periods and assessing trends in our underlying businesses. Other companies may calculate these non-GAAP financial measures differently, and therefore our measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. These non-GAAP financial measures should only be used as supplemental measures of our operating performance.
Adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net income and adjusted EPS include adjustments for transaction and integration costs, as well as restructuring costs and other adjustments as set forth in the attached tables. Management uses these non-GAAP financial measures in making financial, operating and planning decisions and evaluating RXO’s ongoing performance.
We believe that adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA margin improve comparability from period to period by removing the impact of our capital structure (interest and financing expenses), asset base (depreciation and amortization), tax impacts and other adjustments that management has determined do not reflect our core operating activities and thereby assist investors with assessing trends in our underlying business. We believe that adjusted net income and adjusted EPS improve the comparability of our operating results from period to period by removing the impact of certain costs that management has determined do not reflect our core operating activities, including amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets, transaction and integration costs, restructuring costs and other adjustments as set out in the attached tables, and thereby may assist investors with comparisons to prior periods and assessing trends in our underlying business.
