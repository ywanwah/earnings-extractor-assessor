AMERICAN WATER REPORTS SOLID THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
NARROWS 2026 EPS GUIDANCE TO TOP HALF OF RANGE
AFFIRMS LONG-TERM TARGETS
INITIATES STANDALONE 2027 EPS GUIDANCE, REFLECTING GROWTH OF 8%

•Third quarter 2026 GAAP earnings were $1.94 per share, compared to $1.94 per share in 2025; year-to-date 2026 GAAP earnings were $4.56 per share, compared to $4.47 per share in 2025
◦Third quarter 2026 adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings of $2.00 per share, compared to $1.89 per share in the same quarter in 2025 and year-to-date 2026 adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings of $4.63 per share, compared to $4.40 per share in the same period in 2025
•2026 adjusted earnings per share (EPS) guidance range is narrowed to $6.07 to $6.12 from $6.02 to $6.12; long-term targets affirmed
•Initiating standalone 2027 earnings per share guidance range of $6.53 to $6.63
•Significant regulatory execution YTD on several fronts, including in PA and NJ, with new rates in effect in Q3
•2026 capital investment plan of $3.7 billion on track; approximately 81,000 customer connections added from acquisitions through September 30, which includes the Nexus Water systems
•Progress continues on proposed merger with Essential Utilities; three states have approved and settlements have been reached in Pennsylvania, North Carolina and Texas
CAMDEN, N.J., October 28, 2026 - American Water Works Company, Inc. (NYSE: AWK) today reported adjusted earnings for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, of $2.00 per share, compared to $1.89 per share for the same quarter in 2025, and $4.63 per share for the year-to-date period ended September 30, 2026, compared to $4.40 per share for the same period in 2025.
“The company has delivered solid results to date in 2026, including key regulatory outcomes that provide for a reasonable and timely return on the investments made for the benefit of our customers,” said John Griffith, President and CEO of American Water. “Looking forward to 2027 and beyond, we are fully confident that our long record of consistent execution on the plans we set will continue.”

“On the merger front, we continue to work toward closing the merger with Essential Utilities by the end of the first quarter of 2027 as anticipated. We are encouraged by the continued progress we and Essential are making in the regulatory approval process, including reaching settlement agreements in several states, and with our merger integration planning activities.”

Regulated Businesses
In the third quarter of 2026, the Regulated Businesses’ net income was $407 million, compared to $383 million for the same period in 2025. For the first nine months of 2026, the Regulated Businesses’ net income was $946 million, compared to $872 million for the same period in 2025.
Operating revenues increased $60 million and $212 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the same period in 2025. The increase in operating revenues was primarily a result of authorized revenue increases from completed general rate cases and infrastructure proceedings for the recovery of incremental capital and acquisition investments, as well as incremental revenue from closed acquisitions. For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, operating revenues decreased $18 million and $7 million, respectively, from unfavorable weather impacts primarily in the third quarter of 2026 and warm, dry weather primarily in the third quarter of 2025.
Since January 1, 2026, the company has been authorized additional annualized revenues of $284 million, with $179 million from general rate cases and $105 million from infrastructure surcharges. The company has general rate cases in progress in five jurisdictions and has filed for infrastructure surcharges in two jurisdictions, reflecting a total annualized incremental revenue request of $360 million.
Operating expenses were higher by $30 million and $103 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the same periods in 2025, due in part to increased production costs from higher purchased water cost and usage and increased purchased power and chemicals costs, partially offset by a decrease in operating supplies and services costs, primarily from lower technology related costs, and a decrease in maintenance materials and supplies costs. Operating expenses also include depreciation expense, which was higher by $18 million and $60 million in the same periods, due to the increase in capital investment.
Interest expense was higher by $9 million and $32 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the same periods in 2025, as a result of incremental short and long-term debt primarily to fund capital investments and acquisitions.
Dividends
On October 27, 2026, the company’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend payment of $0.8950 per share, payable on December 1, 2026, to shareholders of record as of November 12, 2026.

About American Water
American Water (NYSE: AWK) is the largest regulated water and wastewater utility company in the United States. With a history dating back to 1886 and celebrating 140 years in 2026, We Keep Life Flowing® by providing safe, clean, reliable and affordable drinking water and wastewater services to approximately 14 million people with regulated operations in 14 states and on 19 military installations. American Water’s 7,000 talented professionals leverage their significant expertise and the company’s national size and scale to achieve excellent outcomes for the benefit of customers, employees, investors and other stakeholders.
For more information, visit amwater.com and join American Water on LinkedIn, Facebook, X and Instagram.
