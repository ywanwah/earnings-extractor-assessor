Bright Horizons Family Solutions Reports Financial Results for the Third Quarter of 2026
NEWTON, MA - (BUSINESS WIRE) - October 29, 2026 - Bright Horizons Family Solutions® Inc. (NYSE: BFAM) today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026 and provided revised financial guidance for 2026. Bright Horizons is a leading provider of high-quality early education and child care, comprehensive back-up care solutions, and educational advisory services. Our offerings support both working families and employers’ workforce strategies by supporting their employees across life and career stages, and improving employee recruitment, engagement, productivity, retention, and career advancement.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights (compared to Third Quarter 2025):
•Revenue of $841 million (increase of 5%)
•Loss from operations of $187 million
•Net loss of $231 million and diluted loss per common share of $4.76
Non-GAAP financial measures
•Adjusted EBITDA* of $163 million (increase of 5%)
•Adjusted income from operations* of $130 million (increase of 5%)
•Adjusted net income* of $88 million and diluted adjusted earnings per common share* of $1.80 (decrease of 3% and increase of 15%, respectively)
“Our third quarter results reflect the strength of our diversified portfolio, with revenue up 5%, including 13% growth in back-up care,” said Stephen Kramer, Chief Executive Officer. “This fall, we were proud to celebrate 40 years of partnering with leading employers to support working families. Our commitment to providing safe, nurturing environments and high-quality care and education for the children and families we serve has guided us since our start in 1986, and I am grateful to our global team, whose dedication brings that commitment to life every day.”
Third Quarter 2026 Results
Revenue increased by $38.7 million, or 5%, to $841.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 from the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to growth in back-up care, partially offset by the reductions in revenue from full service centers that have closed over the last 12 months.
Loss from operations was $187.1 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to income from operations of $120.8 million for the third quarter of 2025. The decrease in income from operations is primarily related to impairment losses of $294.0 million within the international full service operations, partially offset by increased service levels and contributions from our back-up care segment. We had a net loss of $230.7 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to net income of $78.6 million for the third quarter of 2025 due to the decrease in income from operations noted above, a higher effective tax rate and higher interest expense. Diluted loss per common share was $4.76 for the third quarter of 2026 compared to earnings per common share of $1.37 for the third quarter of 2025.
In the third quarter of 2026, adjusted EBITDA* increased by $7.2 million, or 5%, to $163.3 million, and adjusted income from operations* increased by $5.7 million, or 5%, to $130.2 million from the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to increased service levels and contributions from the back-up care segment. Adjusted net income* was $87.5 million, a decrease from adjusted net income of $90.1 million in the same period in the prior year, as a result of higher interest expense and an increase to the adjusted effective tax rate, partially offset by the increase in adjusted income from operations noted above. Diluted adjusted earnings per common share* was $1.80 for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $1.57 for the third quarter of 2025.
As of September 30, 2026, the Company operated 979 early education and child care centers with the capacity to serve approximately 112,500 children and their families.
*Adjusted EBITDA, adjusted income from operations, adjusted net income and diluted adjusted earnings per common share are financial measures that are not calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”), which are commonly referred to as “non-GAAP financial measures.” Adjusted EBITDA represents EBITDA (which is net income (loss), as determined in accordance with GAAP, before interest expense, income tax expense, depreciation, and amortization) adjusted to exclude stock-based compensation expense, impairment losses, incremental general liability expense, debt refinancing costs, lease termination costs, and, at times, other non-recurring costs, such as transaction costs. Adjusted income from operations represents income (loss) from operations, as determined in accordance with GAAP, adjusted to exclude impairment losses, incremental general liability expense, debt refinancing costs, lease termination costs, and, at times, other non-recurring costs, such as transaction costs. Adjusted net income represents net income (loss), as determined in accordance with GAAP, adjusted to exclude amortization, stock-based compensation expense, impairment losses, incremental general liability expense, debt refinancing costs, lease termination costs, interest related to pre-acquisition obligations, and, at times, other non-recurring costs, such as transaction costs, and the income tax provision (benefit) thereon. Diluted adjusted earnings per common share is calculated using adjusted net income. These non-GAAP financial measures are more fully described and are reconciled from the respective measures determined under GAAP in “Presentation of Non-GAAP Financial Measures” and the attached table “Bright Horizons Family Solutions Inc. Non-GAAP Reconciliations,” respectively.
Balance Sheet and Liquidity
At September 30, 2026, the Company had $119.2 million of cash and cash equivalents and $436.0 million available for borrowing under its revolving credit facility. In the nine months ended September 30, 2026, we generated $208.1 million of cash from operations, compared to $202.8 million for the same period in 2025, repurchased approximately 7.7 million shares totaling $557.7 million compared to approximately 0.9 million shares totaling $106.0 million for the same period in the prior year, and made net investments totaling $71.8 million, compared to $63.5 million for the same period in the prior year.
2026 Outlook
Based on current trends and expectations, we currently expect fiscal year 2026 revenue to be approximately $3.07 billion and diluted adjusted earnings per common share to be in the range of $4.98 to $5.03. The Company will provide additional information on its outlook during its earnings conference call.

About Bright Horizons Family Solutions Inc.
Bright Horizons® is a leading provider of high-quality early education and child care, back-up care, and workforce education services.
