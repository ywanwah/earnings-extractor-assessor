Coya Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides a Corporate Update

HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Coya Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: COYA) (“Coya” or the “Company”), a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing biologics intended to enhance T-cell (Treg) function in patients with neurodegenerative disorders, announces its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provides a corporate update.

“Completion of enrollment in our Phase 2/3 ALSTARS trial represents a significant achievement for Coya as we advance COYA 302 for the treatment of ALS,” said Arun Swaminathan, Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer of Coya Therapeutics. “With topline data anticipated in early Q2 2027, we remain focused on communicating data from this study as soon as it is available.”

Recent Corporate Highlights

•
Completed enrollment of 120 planned participants in the Phase 2/3 ALSTARS trial of COYA 302 for the treatment of ALS. Coya allowed participants in screening to enter the trial. A total of 137 participants were enrolled.
•
In response to requests from ALSTARS investigators and study participants, Coya announced plans to initiate an open-label extension (OLE) of the ALSTARS trial, providing participants who complete the 48-week study period the opportunity to continue receiving COYA 302.
•
Presented at the 2026 Annual NEALS Meeting Neurofilament Light Chain (NfL) Biomarker Stratification Methodology to Minimize the Impact of Heterogeneity in ALS Clinical Trials
•
Announced the publication of preclinical results for COYA 303 in the International Journal of Molecular Sciences demonstrating complementary immunomodulatory effects in a well-established model of peripheral and central nervous system (CNS) inflammation.

Upcoming Expected Milestones

•
4Q 2026-Q1 2027: Report additional mechanistic data and single-cell proteomics data from the completed ALS and AD investigator-initiated trials, subject to publication
•
Q1 2027: Announce biomarker data from the completed investigator-initiated study in patients with frontotemporal dementia (FTD).
•
Early Q2 2027: Report topline data from 24-week double-blind treatment phase of the Phase 2/3 ALSTARS trial of COYA 302 for the treatment of ALS.
•
2027: Initiate a Phase 2 study evaluating COYA 302 for the treatment of FTD.

“Completion of the ALSTARS trial is an important milestone as we move forward in our quest for providing a potential therapy for this lethal disease,” said Fred Grossman, DO, FAPA, President and Chief Medical Officer of Coya Therapeutics. “The addition of the open-label extension provides

 

 

 

participants the opportunity to continue on COYA 302 while we continue to collect important data.”

 

 

Financial Results

 

As of September 30, 2026, Coya had cash and cash equivalents of $35.8 million, sufficient to fund operations, as currently planned, past the Phase 2/3 ALSTARS topline data readout and into the second half of 2027.

 

R&D Services revenue is calculated quarterly using the inputs approach, by applying actual COYA 302 - ALS expenses against budgeted COYA 302 - ALS expenses as the two inputs. Collaboration revenue of $0.2 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, consisted of R&D Services. Collaboration revenue of $3.6 million during the three months ended September 30, 2025, consisted primarily of the immediate recognition of $3.3 million of License revenue upon receiving FDA acceptance of our IND for the Phase 2/3 ALSTARS Study and $0.3 million of R&D Services.

 

Research and development expenses increased by $5.7 million from $2.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, to $8.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026. The increase was primarily due to a $5.6 million increase in preclinical and clinical product candidate costs, reflecting primarily the advancement of the COYA 302 ALS Phase 2/3 clinical trial. The increase was further driven by a $0.1 million increase in internal research and development expenses.

In-process research and development was $23,240 and $0.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, and 2025, respectively. The $0.5 million was a result of milestone payments pursuant to our license agreements which were due upon the achievement of the FDA's approval of our IND of COYA 302 in patients with ALS. Milestone payments pursuant to our license agreements were not incurred during the three months ended September 30, 2026.

General and administrative expenses were $2.6 million for both the three months ended September 30, 2026, and 2025.

Net loss was $10.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to net loss of $2.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

 

About COYA 302

COYA 302 is an investigational and proprietary biologic combination therapy with a dual immunomodulatory mechanism of action intended to enhance the anti-inflammatory function of regulatory T cells (Tregs) and suppress the inflammation produced by activated monocytes and macrophages. COYA 302 comprises proprietary low dose interleukin-2 (LD IL-2) and CTLA-4 Ig and

 

 

 

is being developed for subcutaneous administration for the treatment of patients with ALS and other neurodegenerative diseases. These mechanisms may have additive or synergistic effects.

COYA 302 is an investigational product not yet approved by the FDA or any other regulatory agency.

 

About ALSTARS

Coya is currently conducting the ALSTARS trial, a Phase 2/3, randomized, multi-center, double-blind, placebo-controlled study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of COYA 302 for the treatment of ALS (Identifier: NCT07161999). Enrollment in the ALSTARS trial is complete, with 137 subjects enrolled. Subjects are randomized in equal proportions to placebo or one of two dosing regimens. In the high-dose regimen, subjects receive both CTLA4-Ig and low-dose IL-2 twice per month, during weeks 1 and 3. In the low-dose regimen, subjects receive CTLA4-Ig in week 1 and low-dose IL-2 during week 3. This low-dose schedule differs from the original design in which both agents were to be administered once per month during week 1. The revised low-dose schedule has been in place from the beginning of the trial. The revised schedule preserves the mechanistic rationale of the original regimen and the total monthly drug exposure in this dosing sequence is the same as the originally intended sequence. The Company believes that this change in dosing sequence will have no impact on any potential efficacy or safety of this dosing regimen.

The trial begins with a 24-week placebo-controlled treatment period, followed by a second blinded 24-week period. During the second period, subjects in the high- and low-dose groups continue their assigned regimens, while subjects initially assigned to placebo are randomized in equal proportions to one of the two active regimens. After the second 24-week period, all subjects will be invited to participate in an ongoing open-label extension, in which they will receive the high-dose regimen.

About Coya Therapeutics, Inc.

Headquartered in Houston, TX, Coya Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: COYA) is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing proprietary treatments focused on the biology and potential therapeutic advantages of regulatory T cells (“Tregs”) to target systemic inflammation and neuroinflammation. Dysfunctional Tregs underlie numerous conditions, including neurodegenerative, metabolic, and autoimmune diseases. This cellular dysfunction may lead to sustained inflammation and oxidative stress resulting in lack of homeostasis of the immune system.

Coya’s investigational product candidate pipeline leverages multiple therapeutic modalities aimed at restoring the anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory functions of Tregs. Coya’s therapeutic platforms include Treg-enhancing biologics, Treg-derived exosomes, and autologous Treg cell therapy.
