Relmada Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

 

●NDV-01 CMC program on track to submit the IND by year-end 2026, with initiation of the registrational Phase 3 SPECTRUM program upon IND clearance

 

●Long-term extension NDV-01 Phase 2 data demonstrate continued durability of response: 86% of 12-month complete responders remained in complete response at 18 months, with no Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events or discontinuations

 

●Registrational Phase 3 program renamed SPECTRUM; two independent registrational pathways in NMIBC remain unchanged

 

●Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of approximately $202.1 million as of September 30, 2026, expected to fund operations through 2029

 

CORAL GABLES, Fla., Nov. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Relmada Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: RLMD, “Relmada” or the “Company”), a clinical-stage biotechnology company advancing innovative therapies for oncology and central nervous system disorders, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update, including 18-month results from the NDV-01 Phase 2 study, the registrational Phase 3 SPECTRUM program, and continued progress toward the planned year-end 2026 Investigational New Drug (IND) submission for NDV-01 and sepranolone.

 

“Relmada has made significant progress in advancing the NDV-01 CMC program and remains on track to submit the NDV-01 IND by year-end 2026. Furthermore, we have established a manufacturing plan to provide uninterrupted clinical supply through the completion of the Phase 3 SPECTRUM program,” said Sergio Traversa, Chief Executive Officer of Relmada Therapeutics.

 

“Initial 18-month results from the ongoing NDV-01 Phase 2 study demonstrate extended durability of complete response and continued favorable tolerability,” added Bipin Dalmia, Chief Business Officer of Relmada Therapeutics. “Our Phase 3 program remains unchanged, with two independent registrational pathways in NMIBC, and we have renamed it SPECTRUM to reflect the potential breadth of NDV-01 across the NMIBC disease spectrum. We expect to begin the SPECTRUM program upon IND clearance.”

 

CMC and Manufacturing Update

 

NDV-01: NDV-01 CMC program is on track for IND submission by year-end 2026, with initiation of the registrational Phase 3 SPECTRUM program upon IND clearance. Furthermore, we have established a manufacturing plan to provide uninterrupted clinical supply through completion of the Phase 3 SPECTRUM program.

 

Sepranolone: The Company is finalizing the stability testing of the pre-filled syringe (PFS) delivery system to enable sepranolone IND submission, which is on track for year-end 2026. We expect to start the Phase 2b trial in Prader-Willi Syndrome upon IND clearance.

 

 

 

NDV-01 Clinical Update

 

Phase 2 Study: Initial 18-month results from the ongoing Phase 2 TRGC-011 study of NDV-01 in high-risk NMIBC continue to support extended durability of NDV-01 complete responses and continued favorable tolerability profile.

 

●Initial 18-month durability results: 23 patients in complete response (CR) at 12 months continued into the long-term extension (LTE). 86% (12/14) of evaluable patients who reached 18 months remained in CR. In the BCG-unresponsive subgroup, 11 patients continued into the LTE, and 88% (7/8) of evaluable patients who reached 18 months remained in CR. Two BCG-naïve patients (T1 at study entry) progressed to muscle-invasive disease (T2a) at the Month 15 assessment.

 

●Updated 12-month efficacy results: Among 51 evaluable patients, the anytime CR rate was 94% (48/51) and the 12-month CR rate was 77% (24/31). In the BCG-unresponsive subgroup, the anytime CR rate was 96% (22/23) and 12-month CR rate was 73% (11/15). Through 12-months, both progression-free and cystectomy-free survival was 100%.

 

●Safety and tolerability: To date, there were no Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events (TRAEs), no treatment-related serious adverse events, and no treatment-related discontinuations.

 

Phase 3 SPECTRUM Program: The NDV-01 Phase 3 program remains unchanged and is designed to pursue two independent registrational pathways in NMIBC:

 

1.A single-arm study in second-line (2L) high-risk BCG-unresponsive carcinoma in situ (CIS) ± Ta/T1 (Cohort 2UC), together with a separate single-arm 2L high-risk BCG-unresponsive Ta/T1 w/o CIS exploratory cohort (Cohort 2UP).

 

2.A randomized study evaluating NDV-01 as adjuvant therapy following transurethral resection of bladder tumor (TURBT) versus observation in low-grade intermediate-risk NMIBC (Cohort IRA).

 

The Company has changed the name of its Phase 3 program from RESCUE to SPECTRUM to better reflect the potential breadth of NDV-01 across the entire NMIBC disease spectrum. Clinical trial sites are engaged and preparing to initiate patient enrollment upon IND clearance.

About NDV-01

 

NDV-01 is a novel, sustained-release, intravesical co-formulation of gemcitabine and docetaxel (Gem/Doce) being developed for the treatment of non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC). The formulation is engineered to provide prolonged bladder retention and sustained drug release for up to 10 days. By forming a soft intravesical matrix, NDV-01 is designed to increase local drug exposure while limiting systemic toxicity. The treatment can be administered conveniently in an office setting in under 5 minutes without the need for anesthesia or specialized equipment. NDV-01 is encompassed by multiple patent applications that if issued, could provide protection until 2047.

 

About the NDV-01 Phase 3 SPECTRUM Registrational Pathways:

 

Relmada has received written FDA feedback confirming alignment on two registrational pathways for NDV-01, including study design, patient populations, and primary endpoints. The Company expects to submit an IND by year-end 2026 and initiate the Phase 3 SPECTRUM program upon IND clearance.

 

About NMIBC

 

NMIBC represents 75-80% of all bladder cancer cases and is associated with high recurrence rates (50–80% over 5 years) and disease progression in a subset of these patients. With 63,000 incident NMIBC cases per year in the U.S. and limited treatment options, the market opportunity is significant. High-risk BCG-unresponsive disease represents the biggest unmet need in NMIBC, with limited and suboptimal bladder-sparing treatment options. While four therapies are FDA-approved for first line BCG-unresponsive disease, there are no approved therapies in the second line or later. Intermediate-risk NMIBC in the adjuvant setting has no currently approved therapies and patients with recurrence must undergo repeated surgical resections. NDV-01 has the potential to serve as a foundational intravesical therapy across the NMIBC disease spectrum.

 

About Sepranolone and GABA Modulation

 

Sepranolone, a synthetic isoallopregnanolone, selectively modulates GABAA receptors by antagonizing allopregnanolone (ALLO), without disrupting GABA signaling. It targets disorders linked to excess GABAergic activity such as Prader-Willi syndrome (PWS), Tourette syndrome, and obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD). More than 335 patients have been treated with sepranolone in clinical trials to date, with a favorable safety profile. The Company is on track to submit the sepranolone IND by year-end 2026 and expects to initiate the Phase 2 study in Prader-Willi syndrome upon IND clearance.

 

About Prader-Willi Syndrome (PWS)

 

PWS is a rare genetic disorder caused by chromosomal deletions on chromosome 15, leading to neurodevelopmental and behavioral complications. Global prevalence is estimated to be 350,000-400,000 patients. Current treatments address symptoms but do not modify the underlying neurobehavioral pathology.
