Datacentrex Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and $30 Million Investment in Eagle LNG
Eagle LNG’s established customer base and space propulsion capabilities create additional growth opportunities heading into 2027
● | Reports positive third-quarter Adjusted EBITDA of $1.3 million, compared with a $1.3 million Adjusted EBITDA loss in Q2
● | $30 million investment adds exposure to an operating LNG supplier serving marine, utility and space propulsion customers
● | Long-term supply contracts and integrated LNG infrastructure provide foundation for potential growth in energy and space propulsion
● | Began energizing prepaid mining fleet expansion after quarter-end, targeting an approximately 21% increase in online Scrypt hashrate upon full deployment
● | Cash and digital assets totaled approximately $29.3 million as of September 30, 2026; no debt
SALT LAKE CITY, November 10, 2026 — Datacentrex, Inc. (“Datacentrex” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: DTCX), a diversified technology-driven enterprise operating a digital asset mining business and investing in energy and space-launch infrastructure, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Third-quarter Adjusted EBITDA was positive at approximately $1.3 million, including $2.2 million of net realized and unrealized gains on digital assets,” said Parker Scott, Chief Executive Officer of Datacentrex. “We also maintained substantial liquidity following our $30 million investment in Eagle LNG, ending September with approximately $29.3 million in cash and digital assets, including $27.5 million in cash, and no outstanding debt. We believe this financial position gives us flexibility to support our mining operations and pursue opportunities we believe can create long-term stockholder value.”
“Our third quarter also included a significant investment in the long-term development of Datacentrex,” said Parker Scott, Chief Executive Officer of Datacentrex. “Our $30 million investment in Eagle LNG gives us an ownership position in an operating energy business with established infrastructure and customers under long-term supply agreements. As we approach 2027, we believe Eagle’s marine and Caribbean energy business provides a foundation for growth, while its ability to supply aerospace-specification liquid methane creates additional opportunity as the next generation of commercial launch vehicles enters service and scales.”

Eagle LNG Investment Strategic Rationale and 2027 Overview
Datacentrex believes its investment in Eagle LNG complements its digital asset operations by adding exposure to established energy infrastructure with growth opportunities in marine fuel, Caribbean power generation and space propulsion.
Key investment considerations include:
● | Established operations and customers. Eagle has produced more than 300 million gallons of LNG since 2018 and completed more than 750 marine bunkering operations without incident. Supply agreements include terms of 10 years or longer and take-or-pay provisions, with certain customers renewing early and increasing contracted volumes.
● | Integrated infrastructure. Eagle’s liquefaction, storage and delivery capabilities support customers requiring reliable fuel supply and specialized logistics, helping it retain customers and compete for additional business.
● | Capacity expansion opportunities. Existing production constraints limit Eagle’s ability to meet additional marine and Caribbean demand. Added capacity, including its permitted Jacksonville export facility in development, could support growth, subject to financing and execution.
● | Space propulsion demand. Eagle is a qualified supplier of aerospace-specification liquid methane for next-generation commercial launch vehicles. Its growth opportunity depends on customer programs, vehicle readiness and launch activity.
“Heading into 2027, we see opportunities for Eagle to expand its established marine and Caribbean business while building its space propulsion business,” Scott said. “Both draw on the same production and delivery capabilities. We believe serving additional demand can strengthen Eagle’s earning power and increase the long-term value of our investment.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
Unaudited
● | Revenue was approximately $1.7 million, compared with approximately $2.4 million in the third quarter of 2025, a decrease of approximately 29%.
● | Gross profit was approximately $80,000, representing a gross margin of 4.6%, compared with approximately $1.5 million and a gross margin of 59.2% in the prior-year period. The decrease reflected lower revenue and higher direct production costs for the larger deployed fleet.

● | Adjusted EBITDA was approximately $1.3 million, compared with approximately $770,000 in the third quarter of 2025 and an Adjusted EBITDA loss of approximately $1.3 million in the second quarter of 2026. Third-quarter 2026 Adjusted EBITDA included approximately $2.2 million of net realized and unrealized digital asset gains.
● | Net Cash Burn was approximately $867,000, compared with net cash generation of approximately $411,000 in the prior-year period and Net Cash Burn of approximately $61,000 in the second quarter of 2026. See the non-GAAP explanations and reconciliations below.
● | Ended September with approximately $27.5 million in cash and cash equivalents and $1.7 million in digital assets, for a combined total of approximately $29.3 million, and no outstanding debt. Stockholders’ equity was approximately $72.6 million.

Third Quarter 2026 Operating and Recent Highlights
● | On August 28, completed the purchase of $30 million of Class A Common Units in ELNG Equity LLC, Eagle LNG’s equity holding company, representing a 10.5% equity interest. An affiliate of The Energy & Minerals Group invested an additional $10 million at the same value per unit.
● | In August, secured colocation capacity for more than 500 additional ElphaPex DG2 Scrypt ASIC miners that had been prepaid in a prior period.
● | At September 30, operated 3,085 Scrypt ASIC miners across four U.S. colocation facilities, with approximately 43.2 TH/s of aggregate nameplate hashrate at full uptime and approximately 12.2 MW of deployed power capacity.
● | On October 14, announced that installation and energization of the additional miners had begun. Upon full energization, the expansion is expected to increase online hashrate from approximately 43.2 TH/s to 52.2 TH/s and deployed power capacity from approximately 12.2 MW to 14 MW, without additional hardware-acquisition expenditures.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This press release includes Adjusted EBITDA and Net Cash Burn, each of which is a non-GAAP financial measure. The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss), adjusted for impacts of interest expense, income tax provision or benefit and depreciation and amortization, and non-cash stock-based compensation. The Company defines Net Cash Burn as net income (loss), adjusted for depreciation and amortization, non-cash stock-based compensation, income tax provision or benefit, and net realized and unrealized gains and losses on digital assets. Unlike Adjusted EBITDA, Net Cash Burn is not adjusted for interest, because management uses Net Cash Burn to assess the periodic cost of sustaining the business after the benefit of interest earned on the Company’s cash balances; that net interest amount is shown as a memorandum line in the reconciliation tables below and should not be added to Net Cash Burn a second time. Both gains and losses on digital assets are excluded from Net Cash Burn symmetrically, and the definitions are applied consistently across the periods presented. Because the Company’s mining revenue is settled in digital assets rather than in cash, Net Cash Burn reflects the combined change in cash and digital assets attributable to operations; it is not a measure of liquidity and is not a substitute for net cash used in operating activities, which was $7,516,521 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. Adjusted EBITDA and Net Cash Burn are not measures calculated in accordance with U.S. GAAP and should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for net income (loss) or any other measure prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Reconciliations of Adjusted EBITDA and Net Cash Burn to net loss, the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP measure, for the three and nine month periods presented are provided in the financial tables included in this press release as net income (loss), adjusted for impacts of interest expense, income tax provision or benefit and depreciation and amortization, and non-cash stock-based compensation.
