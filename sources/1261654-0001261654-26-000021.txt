Universal Technical Institute Reports Fiscal Year 2026 Fourth Quarter and Year-End Results
Company Unveils Next-Generation UTI Campus, Skilled Trades Max, and Details Plan for Up to 12 New Campuses and Approximately 45 New Programs for Fiscal 2027 Through Fiscal 2029
PHOENIX, ARIZ. - November 18, 2026 - Universal Technical Institute, Inc. (NYSE: UTI), a leading workforce solutions provider of transportation, skilled trades and healthcare education programs, reported financial results for the fiscal 2026 fourth quarter and the full year ended September 30, 2026. Universal Technical Institute, Inc. operates in two reportable segments, Universal Technical Institute (UTI) and Concorde Career Colleges (Concorde), and together with its segments and subsidiaries is referred to as the “Company,” “we,” “us” or “our.”
Financial Highlights
•Full year revenue of $897.0 million in 2026, an increase of 7.4% over the prior year.
•Full year net income was $33.6 million, a decrease of 46.7% over the prior year due to strategic growth expenses.
•Full year adjusted EBITDA(1) was $102.0 million, a decrease of 19.3% over the prior year with $34.6 million in EBITDA impact due to strategic growth expenses.
Operational Highlights and North Star Strategy Developments
•Full year average full-time active students of 26,251, an increase of 6.6% over the prior year, with total new student starts of 31,873, an increase of 7.0% over the prior year.
•Introducing Skilled Trades Max, a next-generation UTI campus model with double the skilled trades footprint of the Company’s traditional campuses, with the first campus expected to open in the Midwest in fiscal 2028, pending required regulatory approval.
“Fiscal 2026 was a year of continued growth and progress on our North Star strategy,” said Jerome Grant, CEO of Universal Technical Institute, Inc. “We grew new student starts, average full-time active students and revenue by 7% year-over-year each, with revenue reaching $897 million. Since launching Phase II of North Star at the start of fiscal 2025, we have grown starts 19%, average full-time active students 18% and revenue 22%, all while building more new capacity than in almost any year in our history. In fiscal 2026 alone, we opened three new campuses in Fort Myers, San Antonio and Atlanta, which continue to outpace expectations, and added or expanded 33 programs across our campus network as student demand for skilled trades continued to accelerate.
“Our high school admissions team is fully staffed with approximately 20% more representatives to better serve prospective students and their families, and we are working to add up to 1,000 seats to existing skilled trades programs by the end of fiscal 2027 to serve the accelerating demand. With fiscal 2027 set to be our biggest build year yet, the building blocks for the coming years are in place: up to 12 new campuses, including our first next-generation UTI Skilled Trades Max campus, and approximately 45 new programs and expansions planned from fiscal 2027 through fiscal 2029, in each case subject to required regulatory approval, plus continued growth in our existing business. In total, we are creating capacity for more than 10,000 additional students by fiscal 2029, putting us on a path to support more than 45,000 students. Together, these initiatives give us even greater confidence in our ability to exceed $1.2 billion in revenue and approach $220 million in adjusted EBITDA in fiscal 2029.”
North Star Strategy: Building Toward Fiscal 2029
The Company’s growth through fiscal 2029 is built on three building blocks: new campuses, new programs and capacity expansions, and continued low- to mid-single-digit growth in our existing business.
Fiscal 2027 will be the Company’s largest build year to date with over 15 new and expanded programs and four new campuses planned across both UTI and Concorde, pending required regulatory approvals. The Company also expects to add up to 1,000 additional seats in existing skilled trades programs by the end of fiscal 2027. UTI-Salt Lake City is expected to serve approximately 1,500 students and generate over $40 million in annual revenue at

scale. New Concorde campuses in Houston, Atlanta and the Greater Phoenix area are each expected to serve approximately 600 students and generate over $25 million in revenue at maturity.
The Company plans to launch approximately 30 additional new programs across fiscal 2028 and 2029 and to open four new campuses in each of those years, pending regulatory approvals. Two of the four campuses planned in each of fiscal 2028 and 2029 are expected to be Concorde campuses, and the first UTI Skilled Trades Max campus is expected to open in fiscal 2028. In total, the 12 new campuses and approximately 45 new programs launching over the next three years are expected to add capacity for more than 10,000 students by the end of fiscal 2029.
As these campuses and programs ramp toward maturity, they are expected to contribute meaningful margin expansion in fiscal 2028 and 2029. Together, these building blocks position the Company to meet its North Star Phase II targets by fiscal 2029.
Financial Results for the Three-Month Period Ended September 30, 2026 Compared to 2025
•Revenues increased 6.0% to $235.9 million, compared to $222.4 million.
•Operating expenses increased 6.2% to $209.6 million, compared to $197.5 million primarily due to the growth in both UTI and Concorde average full-time active students and strategic growth expenses associated with new campus launches and program expansions currently underway or completed over the last year.
•Operating income increased modestly to $26.3 million compared to $25.0 million primarily due to strategic growth expenses.
•Net income was nearly flat at $18.0 million compared to $18.8 million primarily due to strategic growth expenses.
•Basic and diluted earnings per share (EPS) were $0.33 and $0.32, respectively, compared to $0.34.
•Adjusted EBITDA(1) increased 15.5% to $42.5 million, compared to $36.8 million which included $7.0 million in EBITDA impact due to strategic growth investments.
•Average full-time active students increased 6.3%, with total new student starts of 12,513 compared to 12,109.
Financial Results for the Year Ended September 30, 2026 Compared to 2025
•Revenues increased 7.4% to $897.0 million, which was within our updated full-year guidance range of $893 - 900 million, compared to $835.6 million.
•Operating expenses increased 13.2% to $851.5 million, compared to $752.1 million, primarily due to the growth in both UTI and Concorde average full-time active students and costs associated with new campus launches and program expansions currently underway or completed over the last year.
•Operating income decreased 45.4% to $45.5 million compared to $83.5 million primarily due to strategic growth expenses.
•Net income of $33.6 million, which was within our updated full-year guidance range of $32 - 36 million, compared to $63.0 million. The decrease was driven primarily by strategic growth investments and increased depreciation expenses during the year.
•Basic and diluted EPS were $0.61 and $0.60, respectively, compared to $1.16 and $1.13, respectively.
•Adjusted EBITDA(1) decreased 19.3% to $102.0 million due to approximately $34.6 million in EBITDA impact due to strategic growth investments, which was within our updated full-year guidance range of $100 - 103 million.
•Net cash provided by operating activities decreased to $94.2 million compared to $97.3 million.
•Adjusted free cash flow(1) of $(12.6) million which was within our updated full-year guidance range of $(20) - 0 million.
•Full year average full-time active students increased 6.6%, with total new student starts of 31,873, an increase of 7.0% over the prior year.

Balance Sheet and Liquidity
At September 30, 2026, total available liquidity was $320.8 million including cash and cash equivalents, short-term investments, and capacity from our revolving credit facility. Total debt at September 30, 2026 was $114.3 million, including $50.0 million drawn on the revolving credit facility. As of September 30, 2026, the Company incurred $112.9 million of cash capital expenditures ("capex"), driven primarily by investments in new campuses, new programs, and program expansions for both UTI and Concorde, along with spending associated with curriculum and equipment refresh and upgrades, facility and leasehold improvements and IT investments.
“Our fourth quarter results reflect continued growth across the business,” said Bruce Schuman, CFO of Universal Technical Institute, Inc. “Revenue increased 6.0% to $235.9 million, average full-time active students grew 6.3% and adjusted EBITDA increased 15.5% to $42.5 million. For the full year, revenue, net income and adjusted EBITDA were all within our updated guidance ranges, and we ended the year with substantial liquidity, giving us the flexibility to fund the next phase of our North Star build-out."
Fiscal 2027 Financial Outlook
“Looking ahead to fiscal 2027, we expect revenue between $925 million and $945 million, while new student starts are expected to grow 9% at the midpoint,” continued Schuman. Our baseline adjusted EBITDA, excluding planned growth investments, is expected to be between $135 million and $141 million, while reported adjusted EBITDA is expected to be between $100 million and $106 million, inclusive of approximately $35 million of EBITDA impact due to growth investments in four planned new campus openings, new program launches and skilled trades capacity expansions, subject to required regulatory approvals. We view these as disciplined, high-return investments that will temporarily pressure margins while building meaningful earnings power for the years ahead. We will see these returns start to materialize in fiscal 2028, when we expect revenue of more than $1 billion and adjusted EBITDA approaching $150 million, and in fiscal 2029, we expect to surpass $1.2 billion in annual revenue with adjusted EBITDA approaching $220 million.”

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Information
In addition to disclosing financial results that are determined in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP"), the Company also discloses certain non-GAAP financial information in this press release and may similarly disclose non-GAAP financial information on the related conference call. These financial measures are not recognized measures under GAAP and are not intended to be and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP. The Company discloses these non-GAAP financial measures because it believes that they provide investors an additional analytical tool to clarify its results of operations and identify underlying trends. Additionally, the Company believes that these measures may also help investors compare its performance on a consistent basis across time periods. Additional details on our non-GAAP measures and the tables reconciling these measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measure are provided below.
Adjusted EBITDA: The Company defines adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest expense, interest income, income taxes, depreciation and amortization, adjusted for stock-based compensation expense and items not considered normal recurring operations.
Adjusted Free Cash Flow: The Company defines adjusted free cash flow as net cash provided by (used in) operating activities less capital expenditures, adjusted for items not considered normal recurring operations.
Management utilizes adjusted figures as performance measures internally for operating decisions, strategic planning, annual budgeting and forecasting. For the periods presented, our adjustments for items that management does not consider to be normal recurring operations include:
•Acquisition-related costs: We have excluded costs associated with both potential and announced acquisitions to allow for comparable financial results to historical operations and forward-looking guidance.
•Integration-related costs for completed acquisitions: We have excluded integration costs related to business structure realignment and new programs for recent acquisitions to allow for comparable financial results to historical operations and forward-looking guidance. In addition, the nature and amount of such charges vary significantly based on the size and timing of the programs. By excluding the referenced expenses from our non-GAAP financial measures, our management is able to further evaluate our ability to utilize existing assets and estimate their long-term value. Furthermore, our management believes that the adjustment of these items supplements the GAAP information with a measure that can be used to assess the sustainability of our operating performance.
•Restructuring costs: In May 2026, management approved and implemented phase I of a multi-phase restructuring plan across all segments to simplify how we operate, improve student acquisition and better align our resources behind the highest-return opportunities across the business. Phase II was rolled out in July 2026 and is expected to span over the next three years as part of our continued focus on optimization and to better align resources to support our overall growth strategy. In December 2023, we announced plans to consolidate the two Houston, Texas campus locations to align the curriculum, student facing systems, and support services to better serve students seeking careers in in-demand fields. As part of the transition, the MIAT Houston campus began a phased teach-out in May 2024 and began operating under the UTI brand.
To obtain a complete understanding of our performance, these measures should be examined in connection with net income (loss) and net cash provided by (used in) operating activities, determined in accordance with GAAP, as presented in the financial statements and notes thereto included in the annual and quarterly filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”). Because the items excluded from these non-GAAP measures are significant components in understanding and assessing our financial performance under GAAP, these measures should not be considered to be an alternative to net income (loss) or net cash provided by (used in) operating activities as a measure of our operating performance or liquidity. Exclusion of items in the non-GAAP presentation should not be construed as an inference that these items are unusual, infrequent or non-recurring. Other companies, including other companies in the education industry, may define and calculate non-GAAP financial measures differently than we do, limiting their usefulness as a comparative measure across similarly titled performance measures presented by other companies. About Universal Technical Institute, Inc.
Founded in 1965, Universal Technical Institute, Inc. (NYSE: UTI) is a national leader in workforce solutions for transportation, skilled trades, healthcare and dental education programs. The company's industry-aligned programs are offered at 35 campuses nationwide and online under the brands Universal Technical Institute (UTI) and Concorde Career Colleges and include auto/diesel, aviation, welding, HVACR, electrical and energy, allied health, dental, nursing, patient care and diagnostic training.
